### 4.2 The 5 Strategic Outbound Channels

#### Channel 1: The Automated Cold Email Cannon (Baseline Volume)
* **Domain Infrastructure:** 3 secondary domains (`getdisojo.com`, `usedisojo.com`, `disojosystems.com`). Never send from `disojo.in`.
* **Inboxes:** 6 total inboxes (2 per domain on Google Workspace/Zoho), warmed 14 days via Instantly/Smartlead.
* **Sending Limit:** 30 emails/inbox/day = ~180 emails/day = 4,500/month capacity.
* **Target ICP:** B2B service agencies, high-ticket training academies, logistics/distributors with 10–40 employees ($500k–$5M ARR).
* **Anti-Agency Angle:** Never pitch "websites." Pitch operational bottleneck elimination (client onboarding lag, WhatsApp coordination chaos, spreadsheet breakdown).
* **Frictionless CTA:** Ask permission to send a 90-second Loom breakdown instead of asking for a 30-minute calendar call.

#### Channel 2: The "Hiring Signal" Hack (Highest Intent & Pre-Approved Budget)
* **Source:** LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Wellfound.
* **Filter:** Companies hiring for *"Operations Assistant"*, *"CRM Specialist"*, *"Data Entry / Admin Coordinator"*.
* **The Insight:** The business has publicly broadcasted that their manual workflow is broken AND they have an approved **$40k–$60k/year payroll budget**.
* **The Pitch:** Position Disojo's $3,500 one-time custom software asset as the replacement for a recurring $50,000 annual human payroll liability.

#### Channel 3: The "Agency Partner / White-Label" Flywheel (1-to-Many Leverage)
* **Target:** Founders of 10–30 person Marketing, SEO, Paid Ad, and Creative agencies.
* **The Insight:** Marketing agencies frequently get asked by clients for custom portals, automated CRM integrations, and client dashboards, but lack engineering talent.
* **The Play:** Disojo acts as their dedicated, white-label technical engineering arm. The agency resells Disojo's software at their markup, Disojo builds the architecture, and the agency gets a 20% margin. 1 agency partner yields 1–2 deals/month automatically.

#### Channel 4: The High-Ticket Sniper (LinkedIn + 2-Minute Loom Mockup)
* **Volume:** 15 bespoke accounts/week.
* **Execution:** Spot a public operational friction point (broken onboarding, clumsy form, manual booking). Build a 10-minute `#FAF8F5` neo-brutalist interactive UI mockup. Record a 2-minute Loom walking through it.
* **Conversion:** 20%–30% reply rate due to high-status proof of work delivered upfront.

#### Channel 5: The "No-Code Ceiling" Intercept
* **Source:** Reddit (`r/zapier`, `r/airtable`), Make.com / Airtable community forums, X.
* **Trigger:** Businesses complaining about Airtable's 50k record limit, Zapier's $800/mo bill, or crashing spreadsheets.
* **The Pitch:** Seamless migration to dedicated Postgres/Supabase + Next.js custom software.
