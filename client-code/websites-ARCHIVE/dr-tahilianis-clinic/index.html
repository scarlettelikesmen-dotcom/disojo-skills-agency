Consultation guide

What would you like to discuss?

These are broad dermatology concern areas, not a diagnosis or treatment promise. A consultation determines the appropriate next step.

Skin / 01

Acne + breakouts

Consultation for active breakouts, recurring acne and the marks or texture that may follow.

Skin / 02

Pigmentation + tone

Assessment of uneven tone, dark patches and changes in pigmentation before choosing an approach.

Skin / 03

Rashes + irritation

Clinical review of itching, sensitivity, inflammation or a rash that is new, recurring or persistent.

Hair / 04

Hair + scalp concerns

Evaluation of hair fall, thinning and scalp symptoms with attention to possible contributing factors.

Review / 05

Long-term skin conditions

Ongoing dermatology support for conditions that benefit from monitoring and adjusted care over time.

Review / 06

General skin consultation

A starting point when you have noticed a change, have several concerns or are unsure what you need.
