01 / SOURCING

Farm Gate Procurement

Direct harvest acquisition from verified local farmers in the Sangli and Krishna River basin. Bypassing middlemen ensures transparent pricing and unmatched crop traceability.

02 / STORAGE

Raw Material Warehouse

Harvested roots are received at our Sangli facility, sorted by lot number, and stored in our 2000 mt moisture-free depot to preserve native quality.

03 / MILLING 1

Automated Polishing

7,000 MT annual capacity drums remove outer skin and impurities to reveal the bright golden core, producing premium double-polished fingers.

04 / MILLING 2

Ultra-Fine Powdering

Low-heat steel pulverizers grind the fingers into 5,000 MT of export-grade powder annually, retaining all essential volatile oils.

05 / DISPATCH

Logistics & Export

Sealed in airtight, moisture-proof packaging and dispatched globally with certified batch assay analyses. Connected directly to major shipping networks.
