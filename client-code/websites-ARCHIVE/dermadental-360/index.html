Why Isolated Treatments Fall Short

As adults age, natural molar wear gradually reduces the vertical height of the lower face by several millimeters. When this occurs, facial soft tissues begin to fold, creating premature nasolabial creases and jawline slackening. Treating the skin alone with excessive filler leads to unnatural puffiness. At Atelier 360, our dental architects restore the underlying skeletal height first, allowing our aesthetic physicians to tighten the overlying skin naturally and effortlessly.
