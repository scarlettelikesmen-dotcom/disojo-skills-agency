# Taste library

What Tejjo loved and what they hated, in their words, with the work it was about. This is a target to learn from, not a list of laws. One loved site is never a template: TFTL's 96 projects share no look, and Tejjo said "don't make it a stuck system".

## Files
- `websites.md`: sites they approved, sites and builds they rejected, and the moments they named while scrolling.
- `identity.md`: logos, marks and identity rounds. Full log with screenshots: `~/.claude/skills/disojo-identity-lab/references/taste-tejjo.md` and its `shots/`.
- `apps.md`: product and app screens. The thinnest file: ask Tejjo for references before designing an app.
- `content.md`: reels, edits, carousels.
- `studios.md`: what Nonstop, Andrija and Softriver ship, taken apart project by project, with the floor, the bar and the 13 rules we build identities by. Read before any branding job.
- `inbox/`: anything Tejjo drops (links, screenshots) that is not sorted yet. Sort it into the files above with their words.

## How to use it
1. Before the idea: read the file for the field. Look at the actual screenshots or open the sites, do not just read the lines.
2. Before showing anything: put the work beside two or three loved examples at the same size. Would Tejjo say the same words about it? If not, say what is missing instead of sending it.
3. Never turn one example into a rule for every job. Each example says why it worked; the why carries over, the look does not.

## How to add a verdict
Same day, one row: date, what it was (with a path or link to the image), Tejjo's exact words, and my one-line read of why. Their words first; my read is marked as mine. A verdict on a full direction goes here; a project-only detail goes in that project's memory note.
