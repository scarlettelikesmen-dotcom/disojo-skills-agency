 New work for Riverflow

Last month we collaborated with the team at Riverflow to brand their ai startup to standout in a sea of generic AI image tools.

Riverflow uses AI to help brand and design teams generate on-brand visuals that actually match their existing standards. No more outputs that feel disconnected from your campaigns, Riverflow tunes itself to your brand DNA so everything looks like it came from your best shoot.

They wanted the brand to feel less like a developer tool and more like a creative engine. So we designed on the idea of “AI with an eye for creative” and anchored everything around the North Star, Tune AI to your brand.

From there, we developed a system rooted in editorial typography, campaign-grade imagery, and a confident yellow-led palette, positioning Riverflow closer to fashion and beauty than traditional SaaS.
