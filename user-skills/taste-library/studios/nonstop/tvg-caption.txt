 TVG Brand Identity 🐎

When we partnered with TVG, the goal wasn’t to make another “clean” agency identity it was to capture the spirit of a team built on grit, speed, and strategy.

Rooted in their Texas heritage, we built a system that embodies workhorse energy bold, relentless, and unmistakably confident. The horseshoe mascot became a symbol of persistence and momentum, while the typography and color palette were designed to feel as sharp and direct as their approach.

Every element was crafted to reflect how they operate in real life: fast, focused, and impossible to ignore.

The result? A brand that doesn’t whisper credibility.
