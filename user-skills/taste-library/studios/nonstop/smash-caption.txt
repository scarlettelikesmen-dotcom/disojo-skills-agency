 When @judekellyy came to us, Smeash wasn’t even a product yet — just an idea in blueprint form. The challenge was clear: take a vision with no physical presence and turn it into a brand people couldn’t ignore.

We built the identity around bold self-expression. Every design decision was made to align with a fearless, trend-driven audience — the ones who lead style rather than follow it.

The system combined a disruptive green + black palette, a wordmark with built-in negative space impact, and a set of assets designed to demand attention both online and in packaging.

The result? Jude has since blown up on TikTok with nearly 400K followers and turned Smeash into a TikTok Shop bestseller at £15.99. From blueprint to bestseller, this identity became the foundation of a movement.
