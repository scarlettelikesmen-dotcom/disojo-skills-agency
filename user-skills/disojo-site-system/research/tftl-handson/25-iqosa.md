# 25 IQOSA (interior design studio)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/iqosa/sheet.jpg`. Wheel blocked (Framer page), fallback scroll used. Cookie banner covers every frame.

## Stack
**Framer** (generator cd31e1f). SangBleu Sunrise + Helvetica Neue Cyr. backdrop-filter, mask-image. 8 MB. TFTL also ship on Framer.

## Idea
Pale, misty, almost no contrast: "Every space is different. The standard never is." in thin wide caps over a hazy photo, the whole site like a fogged window.

## Moments
1. **Loader:** dark slate with a small mark, then a white screen with the wordmark "iqosa" in a script-like serif.
2. **Hero:** two staggered lines in thin caps, right-shifted second line, over a desaturated resort photo.
3. **"Spaces we design" with big thin numerals 1, 2, 3** as tabs (Residential, Hospitality, Commercial, Social wellness).
4. **"IQOSA worldwide"** over a pale map/sky with region labels floating.
5. **Achievements** as a logo row (awards) on white.
6. **About with a soft 3D stone blob** (grey pebble shape).
7. **Close:** "What shall your space make possible?" over a silver liquid-metal image, then a footer with the wordmark centred and links in two columns.

## What Disojo should take
- **Very low contrast as the brand** works only when the type is big and thin; useful to know for calm brands (Mentable).
- **A staggered two-line hero** where the second line answers the first.
