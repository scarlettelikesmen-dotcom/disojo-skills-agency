# 32 Nabil Issa (interior designer, Miami / Milan / Dubai)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/nabil/sheet.jpg`. Wheel blocked, fallback scroll (page 37,945 px tall).

## Stack
Nuxt + Lenis + GSAP (ScrollSmoother, Observer, MotionPath, SplitText, CustomEase). Gotham Thin / Condensed Thin + Helvetica Neue. Lottie logo with a hover version. Howler. 7.8 MB.

## Idea
A designer who works "bold when needed, quiet when called": black pages, ultra-thin condensed caps set enormous, one copper accent, and rooms seen through slits.

1. **Loader:** a thin arc spinner, then the monogram + NABIL|ISSA.

3. **Cities as vertical words:** MIAMI (copper), MILAN, DUBAI (outlined) rotated 90°, standing like columns.
4. **Collage of room photos** sliding in at different speeds.

6. **Arch outlines** (thin line arches repeated) as a pattern between chapters.
7. **Two-word captions in copper on photos:** "BOLD WHEN NEEDED", "QUIET WHEN CALLED".

9. **Numbers huge and thin, staggered:** 97, 120, 10 with small labels.

11. "Chapter" labels in the bottom bar with a progress line.

## What Disojo should take
- **Vertical words as columns** for places or categories.
- **Two-word paired captions** ("bold when needed / quiet when called") that carry the brand line through the photos.
- **Ink-as-you-read** is now on 5+ sites: our `data-ink` is right.
- **Chapters in the bottom bar** as navigation and progress.
