# Face to Face (13322566869.com), scanned 2026-11-23

A one-person digital producer in Miami who sells design projects to companies. The domain is his phone number. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit (GSAP ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, Draggable + Inertia, SplitText), sections as inits (`initHeroSection`, `initWorksSection`, `initPrioritizeSection`, `initFindingSection`, `initCarousel`). 44 MB. Fonts: Founders Grotesk Light and Medium + Helvetica Neue Thin. Red-orange light, black, white.

## The idea
**The contact is the brand.** The site is named after meeting in person and its address is the phone number. It opens by rolling the number in like a slot counter, and closes on the number set edge to edge with Call / Message / Fax over it.

## Moments
1. Intro: "+13322566869" rolls in digit by digit at the bottom (each digit a vertical reel), then a red-lit portrait with FACE / TO / FACE split around his head.
2. A long problem statement in caps with the key half bold; client problems as small labels hanging on thin vertical lines at different heights.
3. A thin dark bar at the left grows into a full panel ("Next, let's take a look at companies free of these challenges"), opening into the work.
4. Work slider of the TFTL sites he sold (AEXLAB, Tanya Timal, Tommy47, HLE, Yodezeen): he is TFTL's producer.
5. Services as outlined pills that tumble into a loose pile ("Optimize the budget", "Reels for Instagram", "And moooore").
6. A spinning ring of text around "Alright, need someone who can handle it?".
7. **The page loops**: after the number, the hero comes round again and the page continues; there is no bottom.

## What Disojo should take
- For a solo founder: the person's face and direct number are the offer; make the contact the hero and the end.
- An infinite loop page, so the end flows back into the start.
- Problem labels on lines, services as a tumbling pile of pills.
