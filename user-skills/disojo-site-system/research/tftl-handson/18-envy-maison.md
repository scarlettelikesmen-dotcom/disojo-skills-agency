# Envy Maison (app.envymaison.com), scanned 2026-11-23

A luxury real estate app (social network meets listings), pre-launch with a waitlist. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit (GSAP ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, SplitText), inits `initFirstScreenAnimation`, `initScrollAnimation`, `initTrendingSection`. 40 MB. Fonts: PP Editorial New Ultralight (a hairline serif) + Helvetica Neue. Deep blue-grey gradient ground, white.

## The idea
**The phone never leaves.** One phone is pinned in the centre for the whole page; scrolling plays the app inside it (a listing, an agent profile, search), and the copy changes either side of it in a hairline serif, one sentence at a time, left then right.

## Moments
1. Intro: the phone with a listing rises; small listing cards float around it at different depths.
2. Sentences hand over beside the phone: the outgoing line fades letter by letter while the next writes in ("Immerse yourself in a world of luxury" / "Ascend to the rank of a leading agent in your region").
3. **Listing cards burst out of the phone** into a 3D cloud of photos with prices, then fall back into depth, blurred, behind "Dive deeper into luxury property's world".
4. "Download the app for your device" closes over the same phone. Phone layout stacks the same story.

## What Disojo should take (direct use: Mentable)
- For an app: pin one device and let the scroll drive the app inside it; the text is the only thing that changes around it.
- Let the product's content leave the screen into the page (cards flying out of the phone) for the one big moment.
- Hairline serif against a technical product to make an app feel like a luxury object.
