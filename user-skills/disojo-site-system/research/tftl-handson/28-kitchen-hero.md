# 28 Kitchen Hero (meal-kit boxes, Ukraine)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/kitchen-hero/sheet.jpg`.

## Stack
**Tilda** (Zero Block, `data-field-*`) + jQuery. Stag Sans. No GSAP. 9 MB. TFTL ship on Tilda too, so the craft is not the platform.

## Idea
The box is the hero: an orange box with a hand lifting the lid, on mint. Everything after is a cheerful order form with hand-drawn line characters.

## Moments
1. **Hero:** the real product box photographed open, on flat mint, headline with a handwritten "super" above "hero". One button.
2. **"How does it work?"** in huge white on mint, with a single hand-drawn line looping off the edge, then three steps as line characters joined by that line.
3. **Order builder in page:** pick portions, days, meals; a table of breakfast/lunch/dinner in pastel columns with prices; dishes as round plate photos from above.
4. **Colour blocks for the story:** yellow and coral halves with the box and a chili, "how this super-story began" with a struck-through word.
5. **The chef** with a quoted line in coral italic.
6. **Ingredients falling** (egg, pasta, tomato) into a pan, steps numbered on the right.
7. **Banner "Don't go hungry, go to us"** in yellow with "YUM!" stickers.
8. **Contact** in two cards, a line character peeking up from the bottom edge.

## What Disojo should take
- **The product in the hand, opening**, as the first screen of any box/kit business. Same as "object doing its real action".
- **One continuous hand-drawn line** that joins the steps across the page.
- **The order builder on the home page** when ordering is the whole point.

## Honest
A 2019-era Tilda site: plain motion, rectangle buttons. Here for range, not for craft.
