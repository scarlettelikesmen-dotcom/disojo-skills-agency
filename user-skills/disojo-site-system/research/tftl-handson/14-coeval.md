# Coeval Studio (coevalstudio.com), scanned 2026-11-23

Architecture, interiors and branding studio, Dallas and Miami (hospitality work: airport lounges, bars). Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit + three.js (KTX2, RGBE, Draco, InstancedMesh, video and Lottie textures, `initEffectShell`: a WebGL layer for image and video effects), GSAP ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, SplitText. Attributes `data-anim-text`, `data-rotate-title`, `data-cursor-hover`. Fonts: Sometimes Times (a light, high-contrast serif) + GT America.

## What it does
1. Loader on a tan ground: one large light-serif percentage, 1% to 100%.
2. **Venetian-blind wipe**: the tan splits into vertical slats that turn away to reveal the work behind (a reveal shaped like the studio's subject, interiors).
3. Headings set with words pushed apart across the width ("Our ........ Works (58)", "What .... We Create"), the count of projects as part of the heading.
4. Work as offset image pairs of different sizes with thin serif captions, colour photos of red-lit bars and lounges against white.
5. Leaders as small portraits with a giant faint word behind.
6. Close: "We'd love to hear" with a one-line email field, then "From You" set huge on navy over a dashed rule and the footer.

## What Disojo should take
- A wipe made of the subject's own element (blinds, slats, louvres for interiors and architecture).
- The project count in the heading.
- A sentence that spans the close: half on one screen ("We'd love to hear"), half on the footer ("From You").
