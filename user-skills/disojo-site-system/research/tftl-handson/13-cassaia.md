# Cassaia Studio (cassaia.com), scanned 2026-11-23

Luxury architecture and interior design studio, Washington DC / Dallas. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit (GSAP ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, SplitText, Swiper, Simplebar), components named `ParallaxImagesAnimation`, `HeaderAnimation`, `SwitchArrow`, `ProjectsList`, `initCircle`. 32 MB. Fonts: **Ogg** (a calligraphic display serif) + Moderat + Moderat Mono for small labels.

## What it does
1. Intro: a small mono line on black, then the room photo rises behind and the wordmark "cassaïa studio" in Ogg slides in huge across the bottom (the diaeresis is part of the mark).
2. **Statement half inked**: "Collaboration is at the heart of our process..." in dark ink, the rest of the sentence faint and filling as you scroll (the ink-as-you-read device again, fourth site).
3. Grounds change by section: charcoal, warm paper, olive green; photos placed in offset pairs at different sizes, small mono captions.
4. A round stamp with the name stacked "ca / ssa / ïa" as a seal on a product photo.
5. "Luxury is in the details" over a full-bleed close-up, a thin vertical line dropping to the Instagram handle.
6. Footer: the name stacked in three lines as the logo, newsletter line, cities.
