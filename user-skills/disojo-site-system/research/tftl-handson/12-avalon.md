# Avalon (avalon.com.ua), scanned 2026-11-23

A residential developer in Lviv selling apartments in several complexes. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt 2 (older), GSAP (ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, MotionPath, Draggable), Swiper everywhere, a map with marker captions (`initMap`, `createMarkerCaption`), 13 videos (a 3D yard fly-through, an intro man, a rebrand film), 91 MB. One face: Graphik in regular. Charcoal grey, white, one blue only in the cookie bar (Cookiebot, not designed).

## What it does
1. Loader: a percentage with a handwritten flourish drawing next to it, the tree mark below.
2. Hero photo of the building with a sentence in light caps, the second line indented.
3. **A handwritten signature stroke** drawn across sections (the founder's line, "life philosophy"), with a signed letter from the founder.
4. A filter panel to search apartments (rooms, floor, price) beside a dark photo.
5. Project cards with prices, a pyramid-shaped building render as a slider, key numbers (12 years, 18 projects, 7.5k, 570k m²), services, news, a consultation form with a hand reaching in.
6. The footer: phone number huge, a brochure ("Selected apartments") standing as an object.
