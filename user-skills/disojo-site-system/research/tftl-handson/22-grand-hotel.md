# 22 Grand Hotel Lviv (hotel since 1894)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/grand-hotel/sheet.jpg`.

## Stack
Nuxt + GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText, DrawSVG, Observer) + PIXI (a WebGL wave slider, `initWaveSlider`). Helvetica Neue Cyr + GT Sectra. AudioContext (sound). Chat widget. 88 MB.

## Idea
An old grand hotel run like a theatre: a red stage curtain opens on the welcome, then the rooms, spa, events and casino follow like acts, each in its own colour ground (stone, burgundy, charcoal, gold).

## Moments
1. **Loader = the hotel's seal:** "GRAND HOTEL LVIV EST 1894" in an oval outline, grey on charcoal, slowly inking in.
2. **Hero on a red velvet curtain video** with the current event ("New Year's Eve 2027, Monte-Carlo Casino Royale") set in a script and serif. The hero is an event poster, changed per season.
3. **"WE ARE NOT JUST A HOTEL, WE ARE PART OF YOUR STORY."** over an old postcard photo of the building. History as proof.
4. **Rooms as a horizontal strip** of photo cards with names and sizes, on stone ground; a round "drag" cursor.
5. **Each department changes the ground colour**: spa dark blue photo full bleed, events on stone with a marquee "STAY WITH US. SPEND AN ATMOSPHERE", groups and spaces on burgundy, restaurant on charcoal, offers on gold.
6. **Two-line statement with the second line ghosted:** "WE STRIVE TO MAKE THE TIME SPENT AT THE HOTEL / EXCEED YOUR EXPECTATIONS" (second line in grey).
7. **Casino as a big arc** (an SVG circle line drawn behind) with the room photo.
8. **Footer:** a full sitemap in columns on charcoal, newsletter top left, and "DEVELOPED BY THE FIRST THE LAST" letter-spaced across the full width as the last line.
9. **Phone:** same acts stacked, the curtain hero kept.

## What Disojo should take
- **Ground colour per department** for a business with many parts (hotel, school, clinic): the colour tells you where you are without a nav.
- **The hero as a seasonal poster** the owner can swap: real reason for repeat visits.
- **The founding seal as the loader** for any business with a real year.
- **A full sitemap footer** is right when there are many pages; it is not lazy if set well.
