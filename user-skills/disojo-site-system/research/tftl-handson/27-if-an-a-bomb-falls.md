# 27 If An A-Bomb Falls (survival guide, non-commercial)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/if-an-a-bomb-falls/sheet.jpg`. Wheel blocked, fallback scroll. First 5 frames black (intro waits for a click or plays a long dark opening).

## Stack
Nuxt + GSAP (ScrollSmoother, Observer, Draggable, MotionPath, SplitText, containerAnimation). Serial A Output (a stencil/typewriter display face) + Neue Montreal. Video. 189 MB.

## Idea
A civil-defence pamphlet. Each scroll is one instruction ("Get inside the nearest building", "Stay inside for 24 hours") in a condensed stencil face, with line-drawn pictograms like an emergency leaflet, on rust, black and paper grounds.

## Moments
1. **Phone opens on a full-screen mushroom cloud** (desktop opens in black).
2. **Instructions as huge condensed stencil caps**, ghosted second lines in grey ("After the fallout arrived").
3. **Line pictograms drawn over photos**: a tap, a gas mask face, a backpack, a TV with an antenna. Archive photos cut in as torn-edge pieces.
4. **A message bubble** ("Is there no connection?") appearing inside the type, phone-chat style.
5. **Ticker of words across a paper strip:** "SAFE. EXPLORE. RADIATION. DAREDEVIL. NOTIF..."
6. **A rotating dial** (a radiation meter scale, curved type around a circle) as section divider.
7. **"Read more" through a scope:** a circular lens with curved text "ALL ABOUT WEAPONS" around it, a blurred photo inside.
8. **Footer:** black, giant outlined title behind, a sitemap of all instructions, three line-drawn heads.
9. **"All about the Gonnads weapons"** pill fixed bottom left on every screen: a persistent second path.

## What Disojo should take
- **The client's own document genre as the design system** (a pamphlet, a manual, a form). For CTESCOA we did the drawing sheet; this is the same move done fully.
- **Pictograms drawn as lines over photos** instead of icons.
- **A fixed pill for the one other thing** a visitor may want, on every screen.
