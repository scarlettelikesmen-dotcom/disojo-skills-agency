# Duck English (duck.school), scanned 2026-11-23

An online English school (Russian-speaking market). Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit (GSAP SplitText, ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer), Lottie, Howler, `getPointAtLength` (text and objects along a path), init names `createDucks`, `animateSVGPath`, `animateEllipseLabel`, `animateSplitTitle`, `animateCircle`. 3.3 MB only. Fonts: Kudryashev Display (a light Didone) + Open Sans. Warm off-white, black, one acid lime.

## The idea
**A duck with a crown.** An engraved (etching style) duck wearing a lime crown is the mascot; two black dots (the duck's eyes) recur as a punctuation mark on every screen. Serious editorial serif against a silly mascot and stickers: the school is playful but proper.

## Moments
1. Loader: numbers scattered on a big pale circle, counting, then the headline in the Didone with lime pill stickers ("CLICK ON ME!", "KEEP IT UP!", "EDUCATION") lying at angles on it; the last word's letters drop out of line.
2. The duck walks in large, then shrinks into the middle of the next title ("Learning English online") as the word wraps around it.
3. A half circle of pale discs as a ground motif.
4. Student stories as white and lime cards with a huge initial letter and a photo, sliding in a tilted row.
5. A sentence set along an arc (text on an ellipse path) turning as you scroll.
6. Blog posts as lime and white cards that fall into a loose pile.
7. Close: the opening headline returns with its stickers, "Let's learn with us", the two dots.

## What Disojo should take
- A mascot drawn in a serious print style (engraving) so it stays premium.
- A tiny recurring mark (two dots) as the site's punctuation.
- Stickers on headlines for a playful brand; text on an arc as a scroll device.
- Proof (student stories) as dealt cards, the second site doing it this way after DaVincii.
