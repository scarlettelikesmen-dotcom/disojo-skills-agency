# 29 Junkineering (mobile robot RPG game)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/junkeenering/sheet.jpg`.

## Stack
Hand-written, no GSAP. Metrik + Camera fonts. `data-speed` (parallax layers). Sprite PNGs (screws, bolts) floating. 34 MB.

## Idea
The game's junkyard world: characters and junk parts fly past as parallax layers, the copy is a pitch sentence with key words lit in acid yellow-green.

## Moments
1. **Loader:** a tiny robot head spinning inside a thin orbit circle, a dot running on the circle as the progress.
2. **Hero:** full key art (pink bear with a blaster), store badges bottom left, "Play video" card bottom right.
3. **Story as one justified paragraph** with words highlighted in the acid colour (Teddy bear, build, robots, save the world). Screws and bolts drift past at different speeds.
4. **A framed card grows into the screen** around a small TV-headed robot (a zoom into a box).
5. **Two lines of huge type sliding opposite ways:** "TURN-BASED RPG ROBOTS COLLECTION" and "MOBILE FREE-TO-PLAY", over a dark workshop scene.
6. **Workshop scene pinned** with hotspots (Base of operations, Robot's inventory, Construction platform) lighting up one by one as you scroll, each with a small caption card.
7. **Diagonal acid-yellow band** cuts in with the robot cards grid (collectible cards with rarity colours).
8. **"Robots upgrading"** with a big robot and cards.
9. **Phone:** reflowed, cards grid, footer with "Made with love in Coleplay games" and a ticker at the very bottom.
