## Idea
"A new standard of living": a calm developer site where light itself is the material. Leaf shadows drift over cream pages; renders of the buildings play as scroll films.

1. **Loader with the headline words scattered** across the dark screen ("A New / Standard / Of / Living"), a small image window between them. The words then fly into place: a scattered sentence that assembles.
2. **Macro hero video:** shells and sand up close, the brand name over it. Starting with a material, not the building.
3. **"Creators of Refined Living"** serif over a palm-shadow photo, dark panel with the paragraph.
4. **Words split to the screen edges:** "Exceptional ... Spaces for Refined Living", "Creating ... Generational ... Spaces" set at three corners of a full-bleed render. Same trick as the loader, carried through the page.
5. **Projects as scroll films** (frame player): the camera moves along a building, a car arrives, a tennis ball drops. Short captions ("Project S", "Project J", "The Den").
6. **Cream page with leaf shadows moving** (alpha WebM over the page) and the architect's pencil sketch: "Architecture for a Bespoke Life".
7. **"The Opportunity" with a numbered tab list** (Flip between images).
8. **Quote over blue sky render**, serif.
9. **Footer:** dark brown, huge outlined "LENVAL" sunk at the bottom, Home / About / Projects / Contact in serif, local time (`data-current-time`).

## What Disojo should take
- **Scattered words that assemble** as the loader and then as the section headings: one device, used all the way.
- **Moving light (leaf shadow video over the page)** as atmosphere: cheap, very real, fits any place-based brand.
- **The Slater kit is the kit to match.** Our kernel already has its eases; add the frame player and the scattered-words heading.
