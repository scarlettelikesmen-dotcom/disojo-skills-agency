# 34 Pictoric (Ukrainian illustration curators, boutique, contest)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/pictoric/sheet.jpg`.

## Stack
Nuxt + GSAP (ScrollSmoother, Observer, DrawSVG, SplitText, CustomEase) + a small WebGL shader. GT America (one family, heavy). Howler. 18 MB.

## Idea
"A world where illustration speaks up": the site is a sunny illustrated land (yellow hills, a blue spiky creature) and the type is heavy, chocolate-brown, shouting.

## Moments
1. **Loader:** white circles bubbling up and merging, a counter "%61 ... %100" (percent sign first, playful).
2. **Headline repeating** "A WORLD WHERE ILLUSTRATION SPEAKS UP" stacking as you scroll.
3. **Projects as a fan of art cards** on the sides with a yellow "OPEN" tag in the middle.
4. **Huge ghost words behind** ("OUR HERITAGE", "ABOUT US", "CALLING ALL") in white over yellow, the real heading small and dark on top.
5. **Illustrated landscape scenes** between sections, the creature reappearing (lying down at the end).
6. **"PICTORIC LAND"** in giant outline type with yellow circles over a photo of their exhibition stand.
7. **Star-burst stickers as buttons** ("MORE INFO", "ABOUT US", "BOUTIQUE"): a spiky yellow shape with the label.
8. **Boutique:** a row of artworks, one tilted and lifting.
9. **Footer:** the creature sleeping on a hill, a fade to white, and "PICTORIC" huge in a hand-drawn outline.

## What Disojo should take
- **Buttons as stickers in the brand's own shape** (the star burst). Answers "no rectangle CTAs" in a playful brand.
- **A mascot that comes back and ends the page asleep.** The page close as the end of a little story.
- **Ghost word behind, real heading on top**: two sizes of the same thought.
