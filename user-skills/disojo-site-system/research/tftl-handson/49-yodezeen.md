## Stack
Nuxt + Lenis + GSAP (ScrollSmoother, Observer, MotionPath, CustomEase). Neue Haas Display + Forma DJR Banner + DIN Pro Cond. A 250 MB page: one long film, plus many large images.

## Idea
One project (Monsoon, Mumbai) is told as a film: the title stays fixed in the middle of the screen while 17 full-bleed frames change under it: water, a door, fire, a hand on a tree, arches, a fountain, rain on a pool.

## Moments
1. **Loader:** a tiny ring on pale grey, then a jungle video with "YODEZEEN" landing in the middle and a "View project" pill.
2. **The statement assembles from scattered words:** "WE ... IN EVERY DETAIL", "STRIVE FOR PERFECTION" with the unfinished words ghosted, a small ring cursor in the middle.
3. **Founders portrait in black and white sliding in** beside the about text.
4. **"MONSOON" fixed centre, frames change under it** on every scroll step. Each frame is a mood, not a room: fire, water, bark, stone. The project title never moves.
5. **Phone close:** the cities stacked huge and tight ("LONDON MIAMI DUBAI WARSAW KYIV MILAN") as the footer, legal text and round social icons under it.
