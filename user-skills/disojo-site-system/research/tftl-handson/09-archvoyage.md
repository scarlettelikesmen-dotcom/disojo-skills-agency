# Archvoyage (archvoyage.ae), scanned 2026-11-23

Architecture, development and interiors, Dubai. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit again (GSAP ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, CustomEase, DrawSVG, SplitText, Swiper, a drag cursor). 58 MB: a 4K Dubai drone film is the hero. Fonts: Neue Montreal + Suisse Intl.

## What it does
1. Loader: a 00 to 100 counter bottom right while the words of the business cycle in (Architecture, Development...), then the drone film of the skyline with the current word as the title, upright caps plus the name in light italic caps.
2. **Mixed upright and italic in one heading** ("OUR *PROJECTS*", "OUR *EXPERTISE*", "SAY *HELLO*") as the type device across the site.
3. A sea-surface film full bleed between sections ("Welcome to Archvoyage" over water).
4. Expertise as an accordion list, awards as a centred list.
5. Closing: "SAY HELLO" huge, then a giant italic sentence with a "SEND REQUEST" link inside the sentence, a marquee of services, and three email columns.
