# Brave Action Ukraine (braveaction.org), scanned 2026-11-23

A platform that publishes everyday people's stories of the war in Ukraine and funds them. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit (GSAP ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, CustomEase, DrawSVG, SplitText, Swiper), 11 videos, 62 MB. Inits read like a named motion vocabulary: `animateTitle`, `animateText`, `animateLines`, `animateHTMLLines`, `animatePhotoUp`, `animatePhotoScale`, `animatePhotoFadeScale`, `animatePhotoRotate`, `animatePhotoScroll`, `animateFromRight`, `initHorizontalScrollElementAnimations`, `initTimer` (a day counter, "DAY 1394"), `initPreloader`. Fonts: Right Grotesk (condensed black) + Neue Montreal. Black, white, Ukrainian yellow.

## The idea
**Ordinary witnesses, shown the way they recorded it.** A painted (watercolour) figure in a yellow coat walking through a ruined street is the hero; the stories arrive as phone screens with the real vertical videos inside, travelling along a thin line, joined by round portrait dots.

## Moments
1. Loader "[000%]" in brackets, then the painting; BRAVE / ACTION / UKRAINE set huge in three staggered lines over it, the words sliding apart as you scroll.
2. "CONNECTING..." whose letters fall out of place and away (each char on its own path), handing over to a horizontal walk.
3. Horizontal walk: phone mockups with real footage (Supporting, Honoring...), a line through them with a round portrait that travels along it.
4. Big condensed statements split around a full-bleed photo ("WE PROVIDE POSSIBILITY / FOR UKRAINE'S FREEDOM") with a yellow "FUNDING NOW" panel sliding in.
5. "WAR (O) STORIES": a round photo set inside the word as its O.
6. A day counter in the top bar. Phone is a careful re-layout, not a squeeze.

## What Disojo should take
- Show proof in the medium it was made in (phone videos inside phones, a contract on paper, a sketch in a sketchbook).
- A round photo inside a letter of a heading.
- A live counter of something real (days, orders, students) when it is true.
- A named motion vocabulary per element type (title, lines, photo up, photo scale, photo rotate): we have reveals for text, we lack named photo motions.
