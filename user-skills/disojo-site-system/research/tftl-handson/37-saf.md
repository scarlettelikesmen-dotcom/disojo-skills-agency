# 37 SAF (architecture and interior studio, Cairo)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/saf/sheet.jpg`.

## Stack
Webflow + Lenis + GSAP (ScrollSmoother, Observer, CustomEase) + Lottie letters (`S.json`, `A.json`, `F.json`). Tosh A (wide grotesk) + Inter Display. 25 MB.

## Idea
A terracotta wall of vertical ribs (a gradient that reads like a lit fluted wall) is the ground of every screen. Wide heavy caps in pale ice blue sit on it.

## Moments
1. **Loader spells the name with three Lottie letters** S, A, F appearing at different points of the screen, then the hero headline lands.
2. **Ribbed terracotta ground** stays fixed; everything scrolls over it. One texture = the brand.
3. **Projects as photo tiles that rise over the ghosted headline:** the big hero type stays behind, darkened, while project photos slide up in a loose grid with captions.
4. **Headline right-aligned in ice blue** ("Architecture & interior redefined"), then the about text small at bottom left.
5. **Their sign photographed on a wall** (the SAF logo in metal with a leaf shadow) as the about image.
6. **A long quote as ink-as-you-read in huge wide caps**, the unread part dark brown on brown. Fills several screens.
7. **Services as pill tabs** (Architecture & Landscape, Residential, Commercial...) with a counter "001 /5" and the heading over a photo row.
8. **Phone:** keeps the ribs; contact as a tidy two-column list (Email, Phone, socials, Address).

## What Disojo should take
- **One material texture as the fixed ground** of the whole site (a fluted wall here). For a maker of a material it IS the brand.
- **The name built letter by letter at the corners** as the loader.
- **The client's sign on their own wall** as the about photo: proof the place exists.

## Honest
Scanner only. Cookie card on every frame. The ribbed ground may be an image or a CSS gradient; not checked.
