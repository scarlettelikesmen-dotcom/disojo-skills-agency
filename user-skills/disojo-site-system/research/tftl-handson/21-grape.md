# 21 Grape (brand marketing agency, Kyiv)
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/grape/sheet.jpg`.

## Stack
Nuxt + GSAP (ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, Flip, Draggable, Inertia, DrawSVG, SplitText, containerAnimation). One font, PP Neue Montreal. Swiper. Vimeo showreel. 150 MB (videos). `data-cursor`, `data-cursor-text`.

## Idea
One colour (periwinkle violet) and one face, nothing else. The agency's name is a cluster of dots (a bunch of grapes), and that cluster is the only graphic on the whole site.

## Moments
1. **Loader as a counter:** full violet screen, "0%" bottom left, "Loading" bottom right, one thin line top left. Plain and confident.
2. **Hero sentence with links inside it:** "Grape® — an independent Ukrainian brand marketing agency". The underlined words are live links in the paragraph, in the violet. The dot cluster sits under it.
3. **Services as a stepped slide** (`initSlides`): "S2 Brand Strategy Explore", a counter "2/4" top right. One service per screen, the screen stays, the text changes.
4. **Client list as huge stacked words** in white on violet: Brave1, Privat Bank, Visa... "+94 more" as a link. The list scrolls through a window; one name at a time is lit.
5. **Work as a two-wide photo grid** with tag chips (Communications, Advertising campaign, Video, 2024).
6. **Awards as a running sentence:** "Effie; Red Dot; Awwwards; The FWA; ..." huge, semicolon-separated, underlined.
7. **Close:** "Let's start creating together." with the dot cluster, contact in small type, then a huge outlined "©2026" sinking into the bottom edge.
8. **Phone:** same sentences, the cluster, a SHOWREEL card. Cookie card stays bottom right on every frame (theirs, not the scanner's).

## What Disojo should take
- **A list set as one sentence** (awards, clients) instead of a logo wall. Cheap, reads as confidence.
- **Links inside the hero sentence** instead of buttons. Fits Tejjo's no-rectangle-CTA rule exactly.
- **The year as the last huge word** of the page.
- **One graphic from the name** (dots = grapes) carried everywhere instead of icons.
