A new beginning

Gifford Hill is South Australia's largest and most ambitious master-planned community in history

Transforming 1,860 hectares into a vibrant home for ~44,000 future residents over the next four decades. This $7.5 billion precinct brings together the ability to live, work, and play and will transform the Murray Bridge region into South Australia’s second city.

A new beginning

Gifford Hill is set to become an economic powerhouse

Creating 7,210 direct jobs and 5,100 indirect jobs. This visionary precinct will add $7.58 billion in economic value to South Australia over its construction and contribute $1.67 billion to the state's GDP annually. Strategically positioned in the golden triangle of employment growth, it will drive strong local economic activity and generate $750 million in annual retail spending.

A new beginning

Sustainability at Its Core

Gifford Hill is dedicated to pioneering sustainable living through the use of mass timber construction, carbon capture, biophilic design principles, and innovative water management systems. Our commitment to environmental regeneration and rewilding in green space will create a model eco-conscious community that dovetails with the natural beauty of Murray Bridge and Adelaide Hills regions.

A new beginning

World-Class Amenities and Infrastructure

Gifford Hill is designed to provide world-class amenities, including a state-of-the-art sports and entertainment precinct, advanced digital infrastructure, and extensive educational offerings. Residents will enjoy a high-quality lifestyle with access to top-tier facilities and services, making Gifford Hill a benchmark for future developments in South Australia.

A new beginning

Fusion of Tradition and Innovation

Gifford Hill celebrates its rich equine heritage while embracing modernity. The integrated racecourse, bridle trails, and top-tier horse and polo facilities alongside cutting-edge digital infrastructure and vibrant town centres offer residents a unique blend of tradition and contemporary amenity, promoting a dynamic and hybrid lifestyle.

Situated just 5 minutes from Murray Bridge, less than 30 minutes from the Adelaide Hills, and under an hour from the Adelaide CBD, Gifford Hill offers the perfect blend of rural tranquility, urban convenience, and cultural diversity.

Over the 30-40 year construction period, the development will bring $9.15 billion in construction investment, injecting $7.58 billion into the region's GDP. This will create 51,280 job years or 12,450 FTE jobs, including onsite full-time positions and indirect employment in the construction and adjacent sectors of Murray Bridge, Mount Barker, Alexandrina, and beyond.

Post-construction, the development will support 12,450 ongoing positions across various sectors, including retail, education, community services, and medical infrastructure. These are estimated to contribute $1.67 billion annually to the economy once fully developed. The Gifford Hill Precinct alone will deliver $750 million in annual retail spending to the local economy upon completion.

Planned for today and built for the future, Gifford Hill embodies forward-thinking principles in urban design, technology, and sustainability. Celebrating the natural beauty of Murray Bridge and the cultural richness of the Adelaide Hills, this community will invite over 44,000 Gifford Hillians to live, work, and play in a place where joy thrives.

Food & Drink

Explore the culinary adventure that awaits you at Gifford Hill. Nestled in the heart of South Australia, Gifford Hill offers the freshest produce sourced from the region’s fertile grounds. This community is a crossroads between the rich culinary diversity of Murray Bridge and the artisanal craftsmanship of Adelaide Hills. Here, food is a unifying element, bringing people from different cultures, backgrounds, and walks of life together. From the local farmers' markets to the boutique cafe, the hole-in-the-wall eatery to the fine dining restaurant – there’s something for everyone, celebrating the spirit and joy that lives here in Gifford Hill.

Botanical

Whether you’re passing by or living in Gifford Hill, there is an opportunity to connect with nature at every turn. Expansive local and district parks, walking, bicycle and bridle trails, and varied botanical gardens are dotted throughout the communities, inviting everyone to breathe and soak in the surroundings. Twenty-one percent of the whole precinct of Gifford Hill is dedicated to nature, providing spaces for the community to engage with, balancing the urban form and serving as a home for a vast array of flora and fauna. Advanced Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD), rewilding, green spines and floating wetlands ensure that the city supports not just a diverse resident population but also a rich plant and animal ecosystem.

Racing and Polo

Gifford Hill provides an exceptional equine experience for those passionate about horses, sports, and entertainment. With over $110 million invested in equine infrastructure in the form of a state-of-the-art racing track and entertainment venue, planned polo grounds, unparalleled racing infrastructure and aquatics – the community nurtures and celebrates the racing DNA of the precinct, racing culture, and the legacy quintessential to the region and the country as a whole.

Diversity

Today, people from every corner of the world and all walks of life piece together the vibrant mosaic of South Australia. A quarter of our residents, born overseas, proudly call this state home. With net migration fuelling the region's growth, Gifford Hill embraces the Welcoming Cities philosophy, championed by the Scanlon Foundation and demonstrated by Murray Bridge. As Gifford Hill extends its embrace toward Adelaide, it commits to creating an environment that celebrates diversity and fosters inclusivity.

Technology

Gifford Hill is committed to investing in world-class digital infrastructure, deploying a fibre optic loop across the precinct. This initiative will enable real-time STEM based research and tools to be delivered in schools and universities, leverage big data and artificial intelligence, and provide high-definition streaming for the sports and entertainment precinct. High-bandwidth internet access to homes will connect and promote and enable the Live, Work, Play ethos of Gifford Hill. Technology will be seamlessly integrated into every aspect of life, transforming Gifford Hill into a tech-enabled precinct and an innovation hub for the future.

We understand that the future of real-time research, entertainment, and the Internet of Things (IoT) will rely heavily on world-class digital infrastructure for households, universities, businesses and sports. From day one, we are committed to delivering this by integrating high-bandwidth, low-latency digital infrastructure alongside the construction of roads, walkways, power, sewer and water systems throughout the precinct. Unlike other growth areas that have omitted this critical infrastructure, Gifford Hill will attract unique, high-value STEM-based businesses and research opportunities that other regions simply cannot provide. Additionally, for those working or studying from home, there will be no limitations, ensuring seamless connectivity and productivity.

The beating heart of Gifford Hill is the entertainment precinct, with the jewel in the crown being the high street. Bookended by the existing $110 million racing club on one end and the mass timber Town Centre on the other, the high street offers a diverse range of eateries, retail shops, and restaurants. Whether you’re out on a Thursday evening with friends, having Friday night drinks after work, or enjoying Sunday morning roaming around the farmers' markets and coffee shops, there’s always something for everyone to enjoy.

The science is clear: the planet is warming. Gifford Hill has the unique advantage of being elevated and connected to one of the greatest water systems in the country – the Murray River. Designed for sustainability and resilience from day one, we are taking practical and meaningful steps from inception to lower our carbon footprint, reduce future urban heat and incorporate systems that preserve and enhance water. This takes many forms. From street trees that provide canopy cover to rewilding open and green spaces to create microclimates and cooling. From water-sensitive urban designs that distribute water through living streams to mass timber urban development that sequesters carbon and offers biophilic benefits. Gifford Hill is a city planned for the future, delivered today.
