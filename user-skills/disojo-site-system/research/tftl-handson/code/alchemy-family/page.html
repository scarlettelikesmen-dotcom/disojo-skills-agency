Our Essence

Inspired by the ancient teachings of alchemy, our club merges modern technology with mystical symbols and artefacts. We celebrate the blend of cutting-edge innovation and timeless wisdom, reflecting our commitment to knowledge and reason.

Our club’s design incorporates enigmatic alchemical elements, creating a distinctive atmosphere. Experience the exceptional fusion of ancient wisdom and contemporary brilliance with us.

HFT
Firms

As pioneers in quantitative trading, HFT firms play a critical role in liquidity provision, market efficiency, and price discovery. The professionals attending from these firms are not only experts in trading but also leaders in financial engineering, data science, and technology, making them key players in shaping the future of finance.

Asset Managers and Hedge Funds

Crypto asset managers and hedge funds are the vanguards of the digital finance revolution, expertly navigating the dynamic world of cryptocurrencies and blockchain technology. Trailblazers in portfolio management, risk assessment, and market analysis, leveraging their deep understanding of both traditional markets and emerging crypto ecosystems.

Proprietary Trading Firms and Pods

Proprietary algorithmic trading firms are the architects of the modern financial landscape, designing and deploying advanced algorithms to execute trades with precision and speed across global markets. These firms operate at the intersection of finance and technology, where data science, quantitative analysis, and machine learning come together to create cutting-edge trading strategies. 

Family Offices and Professional Capital Allocators

The visionaries fueling the future of finance by strategically investing in cutting-edge, algorithm-driven trading systems. These experts combine deep financial acumen with a keen understanding of quantitative models, machine learning, and data analytics to identify and support the most promising algorithmic strategies.

The Fellowship Edicts

01.

Respect All Members

01.

Attend, fair members, and bear in mind that thou art bound by honor to treat all with courtesy, dignity, and grace. Let no ill speech or scornful act mar our fellowship, for such deeds shall find no favor here.

02.

Confidentiality

02.

Whatsoever words are spoken within these hallowed bounds, or what secrets shared, must be held as sacred and inviolable. Let none, under penalty of trust betrayed, reveal to others what doth pass amongst us.

03.

Active Participation

03.

Be not a silent shade, nor one who lingers in the shadows. Rather, with heart and voice, engage in all our doings, lending thy wit and wisdom to our common cause, for in unity lies our strength.

04.

No Self-Promotion

04.

Seek not to advance thyself or thine own cause by cunning speech or sly persuasion within this circle. Our purpose is collective, and the gain of one shall never outweigh the good of all.

05.

Supportive Environment

05.

Let thy words be as balm to those who seek it, and thy counsel given with gentleness. Raise up thy fellows in their quests and labors, and in so doing, may thou also rise.

06.

Follow the Code of Conduct

06.

To every rule, there is a law, and to every law, obedience. Let no man or woman stray from the path laid forth, for the good order of our company rests upon each one’s steadfastness.

07.

Statutory Adherence

07.

Lastly, mark well the laws of the land and the statutes of our fellowship. Whosoever transgresseth these bounds, be it in deed or in spirit, shall find their place here forfeit, and their membership annulled.
