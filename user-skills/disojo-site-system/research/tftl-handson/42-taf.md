# 42 TAF Architecture
Scanned 2026-11-23. Sheet: `scan/taf/sheet.jpg`. Wheel blocked (height 864): the home is a draggable canvas.

## Stack
Nuxt + GSAP (Draggable, Inertia, Observer). PP Formula Condensed + Euclid Circular B. `data-col`, `data-row`: a grid of tiles. `blob_big.mp4`. 12 MB.

## Idea
The whole portfolio is the home: an endless pale field of project photos at loose, uneven sizes that you drag around with inertia, like a pin board or a site plan.

## Moments
1. **Loader:** a black screen with a liquid chrome blob (video) and "TAF ARCHITECTURE" inside it.
2. **Home = drag field:** small project photos scattered with gaps on light grey, each with a tiny caption; drag in any direction, it loops.
3. **Vertical black side rail** with the logo and rotated nav links on the left edge.
4. **Phone:** the field becomes a two-column photo grid.
