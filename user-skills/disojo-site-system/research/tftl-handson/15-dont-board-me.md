# Don't Board Me (dontboardme.com), scanned 2026-11-23

In-home pet sitting and dog walking, San Diego. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit (GSAP ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, Flip, DrawSVG, SplitText), Lottie (`Dog.json`), Howler for sound. 8 MB. Fonts: Bayon (condensed caps) + Neue Montreal. Pink, red, cream, sky blue, a yellow tennis ball.

## The idea
**You play with the dog before you meet the business.** The site opens on a gate: "BOUNCE A BALL TO GET TO THE SITE", a yellow tennis ball rolling across a pink screen. The visitor has to bounce the ball (an interaction, not a timer) to get in. The wheel does nothing until you do; headless scanning stayed on the gate.

## Behind the gate (seen on phone)
- A big hand-drawn shaggy dog in red line art holding its own lead, "A TIRED DOG IS A HAPPY DOG!"
- How it works as numbered steps with flat illustrations, a "Questions?" block in blue with the phone number and tennis balls, price cards ("Singular walks (30 min) from $30"), real photos only in the footer.

## What Disojo should take
- A playful gate that is the brand's own action (throw the ball) for a consumer business where delight sells.
- Risky: it blocks people who just want the price. Use only where the client accepts that, with a skip.

## Honest
Desktop scan stayed on the gate (the wheel does not pass it). Phone frames show the site behind. Not played by hand.
