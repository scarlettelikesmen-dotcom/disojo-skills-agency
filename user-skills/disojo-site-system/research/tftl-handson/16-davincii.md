# DaVincii (davincii.com), scanned 2026-11-23

An "intelligence system" (AI and crypto research platform). Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt kit + three.js (Draco GLB models: `model_draco.glb`, a separate `footer_draco.glb`, RGBE light, physical material, shaders), GSAP (ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, DrawSVG, MorphSVG, SplitText), Lottie (`preloader.json`, `eye.json`), Howler sound. Same eases as ADA (`default-ease .24,1,.36,1`, `easeOutQuint .22,1,.36,1`): one TFTL Nuxt team. Own display font "DaVinci" + Kessler (a serif with italic) + Inter. 10 MB.

## The idea
**An astrolabe that becomes an eye.** The same engraved, esoteric world as Alchemy Family (likely the same designer): charcoal paper, sun and moon faces, compass stars, Renaissance ornament. The tool is presented as an instrument of insight.

## Moments
1. Intro: an astrolabe ring draws itself (DrawSVG), ticks and stars rotate into place, then the ring opens into an eye shape, which opens onto "Where Intelligence Becomes Living".
2. Light falls from the top like a spotlight in a dark room; the logo hangs as a small emblem at the top centre.
3. "Intelligence System" with three engraved discs (sun, compass, moon) as a slider of what it does.
4. **Case studies as tarot cards**: tall dark cards with ornate borders and a round client logo, dealt in a fanned stack that flips forward one by one as you scroll, over "From Vision to Reality: Case Studies".
5. The close: "Connect with DaVincii", then the heading runs as a marquee around an engraved sun, a separate 3D footer scene, a whitepaper card with a QR code.

## What Disojo should take
- Case studies as a deck of cards dealt by scroll: a strong pattern for any list of clients or products.
- An instrument from the subject (astrolabe, compass, dial) as the loader.
- A whole site lit like one dark room with a spotlight from above.
