# 20 Fame Estate (real estate agency, Dubai and Bangkok)
Scanned 2026-11-23 with scan.cjs (desktop wheel + phone). Sheet: `scan/fame-estate/sheet.jpg`.

## Stack
Nuxt (TFTL's second kit). Helvetica Neue + Times New Roman. Attributes `data-dark-screen`, `data-light-screen`, `data-time-place`, `data-scroll-section`, `data-start-trigger`. Howler for sound. About 34 MB loaded.

## Idea
An agency that sells "emotion, a new sense of self". The site plays a short film in panels: lifestyle photo, then a deep red brand panel, then proof, then the office, then a hanging sign.

## Moments
1. **Loader:** black, "FAME" letter-spaced in the middle, small corner labels. Then the hero photo (woman on a terrace) with the headline in caps.
2. **Red panel grows from a dot.** A small red rectangle in the middle of the hero grows to fill the screen; the brand statement sits in it. The colour is the brand, used as a curtain.
3. **Sentence slides sideways.** A Times serif sentence runs horizontally across the screen while the red panel exits to the side. Serif against the Helvetica everywhere else marks the "voice" lines.
4. **Stats strip over a photo.** Huge numbers in a row (35+, 160M, 6, 71%) cut across a blue tower photo, then the same strip slides over the hero photo again. Numbers are the layout.
5. **Cases as a dark photo grid** with "OUR CASES" centred.
6. **Huge caps over a sea photo:** "ZERO COMMISSIONS, EXCLUSIVE ACCESS, AND FULL SUPPORT" fills the screen and scrolls under the frame, with a small tilted image card floating over it.
7. **Partnership section** on pale grey: a guarantee number with an image card tilted in 3D.
8. **Founder on marble:** a portrait card on a black/white marble texture with the name huge.
9. **Close, in three beats:**
   - red screen again, "READY TO FIND YOUR DREAM HEAVEN?" huge, with a short form list
   - "Agency office in Opus Tower" over a dark building photo, a stack of images sliding up the right side
   - footer on flat grey: a 3D extruded "EF" monogram hanging from a pin like a shop sign, contact columns either side
10. **Phone:** its own layout. A "RENT" card pinned on the hero, the services as a stacked serif/caps list over a photo, the hanging monogram again at the end.

## What Disojo should take
- **The brand colour as a curtain that grows from a dot.** Cheap (one scaled box), and it says "this is us" between two photos. Works for any client with one strong colour.
- **The page close as three beats:** the question, the real place (office photo), the sign. The footer is an object from the business (a hanging shop sign), not a list of links.
- **Serif for the voice lines only** inside a grotesk site.

## Honest
Scanner only, no hand scroll. The stats and the red panel timings were read from frames, not code. The monogram may be a 3D render or a CSS 3D object; not checked. Copy is generic agency language; the craft is in the pacing, not the words.
