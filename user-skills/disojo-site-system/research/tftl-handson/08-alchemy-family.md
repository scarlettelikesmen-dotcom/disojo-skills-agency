# Alchemy Family (alchemy.family), scanned 2026-11-23

A private, member-by-application club for fund managers and crypto traders. Study only.

## Stack
Nuxt + GSAP (ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, Observer, CustomEase, MorphSVG, SplitText), a small shader. 11 MB. Fonts: Romie (a sharp display serif, roman and italic) + PP Neue Montreal for labels. **The same Nuxt kit as 4X Strategy and AEXLAB**: TFTL run two code kernels, Webflow + Slater for some clients, Nuxt + GSAP ScrollSmoother for others.

## The idea
**An alchemist's engraved book.** Everything is drawn as old engraving in grey on charcoal: a sunburst with the seal at the top, a huge all-seeing eye framing "Our Noble Quest", rolling clouds, a sun with a face. Monochrome on purpose; colour appears only in the event photos (the Seychelles gathering) and fades back.

## Moments
1. Seal at the top centre of every screen (the logo as a wax seal), sun rays behind the intro sentence.
2. The eye opens around the mission text (engraved eye as the frame for a paragraph).
3. Member types as four tall engraved cards with round emblems.
4. "The Fellowship Edicts": a list of rules where the current line is dark and full size and the others are faint, stepping as you scroll (the same stepped list idea as Jesko's cities), on a parchment ground the clouds roll over.
5. Key persons in round medallion frames, like portraits in an old book.
6. The footer ends on clouds and the seal.

## What Disojo should take
- A whole illustrated world in one print technique and one ink colour, with the client's mark as a seal.
- A list of principles as a stepped, one-line-at-a-time read.
- Portraits framed in a shape from the world (medallions), not plain squares.
- Colour withheld until the real photos, so the photos land.
