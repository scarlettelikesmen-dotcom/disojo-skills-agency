# Disojo site system

One skill, seven stages, one starter. **Load this skill fresh at the start of every new project; a copy from earlier in a session may be out of date.** Replaces the old playbook, intake, archetypes, React starter, scroll rebuild, premium method, redo pipeline, art direction, site flow, premium QA, premium look and launch checklist (archived 2026-11-02 in `~/.claude/skills-archive/2026-11-02/`, not deleted).
Separate skills it calls, each with its own job: `disojo-image-pipeline` (find, tier and grade photos), `disojo-brand-system` / `disojo-logo-design` (only when identity is in scope), `disojo-3d-product-site` and `disojo-packaged-product-3d` (only for a real 3D hero).

`SK=~/.claude/skills/disojo-site-system`. Node scripts run with `NODE_PATH=$HOME/.disojo-tools/node_modules` (puppeteer-core on system Chrome).

## Why it is built this way
**2026-11-09, Kiran Roy.** Left alone I produce the average landing page, and this system was teaching it: it had a hero formula, a section kit and gates that passed the result. Every reference works because one real thing from that client does the work. So the idea comes first, in words, and Tejjomay picks it before any pixel exists. Rules cannot give taste; they only move Tejjomay's judgment to where it is cheap. Reuse the process and the plumbing, never the look.

Measured 2026-11-02 (`references/composition-bands.md`): reference sites carry a big photograph on 3 to 8 of their first 8 screens (median 5), we carried 0 to 2; they run about 50 words and 4 text sizes a screen, we ran 135-151 words and 8.6 sizes. Hence words cut early and photos found early.

## The reference bar (hard rules, added 2026-11-03 after VÆL)
On 2026-11-03 the VÆL demo passed every gate below and was still far under the reference sites; Tejjomay lost trust in it. Gates catch defects only. These three steps are not optional and are never skipped to save time:
1. **Teardown before building.** For any premium, flagship or 3D site, the first output is a measured teardown of the reference sites: `node $SK/scripts/teardown.cjs <project>/research/teardown <url>...` (every file it loads: models, HDRIs, textures, libraries, post-processing) plus `study_site.cjs` screens. Write under each teardown how the hero is made, where the realism comes from, and what we need to match it. Tejjomay sees this before any design or code. A site the script flags as offline is not studied from that run.
2. **Side by side before showing.** Nothing goes to Tejjomay without `node $SK/scripts/side_by_side.cjs <project>/qa/sbs <ourUrl> <refUrl>...` (same viewport, same scroll stops, ours left, reference right; `PHONE=1` adds 390 wide) and every row in its `scores.md` filled in with what they do that we do not. Send the sheet with the work. The hook blocks sending design images without a fresh sheet. I never score who wins and never say it "holds up": Tejjomay decides.
3. **Gate passes are not quality.** premium_gate, system_check and phone_audit are defect checks: they prove "not broken". Never call their result "audited", "premium" or "ready", and never report 0 FAIL as progress.

## The site guard
`hooks/site_guard.cjs` is installed in `~/.claude/settings.json`. **Before:** under `~/Desktop/websites ARCHIVE/*`, for any project with a `brief/IDEA.md` or created since 2026-11-09, design files (astro, css, js, html, json, svg) cannot be written until `PICKED BY TEJJO:` is filled, and design images cannot be sent without a fresh side-by-side sheet in `qa/sbs/`. It cannot prove Tejjomay said the words: never fill the line unless they did. It does not cover files made through Bash or deployed. **After:** on any edit to a site file (astro, css, js, html, json) under `~/Desktop/disojo-mix`, `~/Desktop/disojo-hybrid` or `~/Desktop/websites ARCHIVE/*`, ending the turn is blocked until `premium_gate.cjs` has run for that project. When the gate truly does not apply (a non-visual change, an edit already verified with screenshots this turn), stamp it and say so to Tejjomay: `node $SK/scripts/premium_gate.cjs --skip <root> "<reason>"`. Never skip to save time.

The gate is a defect check and says nothing about quality. What it fails: Inter, Fraunces, Instrument Serif, a one-word accent in the h1, overflow, broken images, images shown soft (real file pixels under 75% of what the screen needs at 2x; reads the srcset file actually chosen), placeholders, em dashes, banned hype words, phone text under 12px, a first screen with neither a photo nor type at scale, fewer than 3 in 8 screens with a big image. It warns on more than one eyebrow per three sections, more than one button in the first screen, and outside the reference bands for words, boxes, buttons and text sizes per screen, no image ever covering half a screen, and under half the big images reading as photographs.

## Hard rules
- **Every website is built in Astro, from the first screen to launch (Tejjomay, 2026-11-09).** No hand-written HTML sites or pages, no loose `index.html` concepts, no Vite, React or Next.js site. Comparison previews are Astro pages too. Enforced: the site guard denies writing `.html` in a project, `premium_gate.cjs` fails a root with no Astro config, `deploy_demo.sh` refuses to publish one. Apps and internal tools are not websites and are outside this rule.
- **Location and a small live clock in the footer of every site (Tejjomay, 2026-11-09).** The client's real city and its local time, or something equally small and real. `BRAND.place` and `BRAND.tz`, `components/FootPlace.astro`; the look comes from the idea. City unknown: `TODO(client)`, never guessed. `premium_gate.cjs` warns if it is missing.
- **Never invent content.** Copy is the client's word for word or Tejjomay's; numbers only when verified; missing facts become `TODO(client)` and go on the gap list. No stock or AI photo next to a client name, result or quote (tiers in `disojo-image-pipeline`).
- **Every client gets its own look, from its own idea.** The library is plumbing and proven mechanisms; never ship a past client's look (`feedback-unique-design-per-client`).
- **Three, always (Tejjomay, 2026-11-08).** Three ideas in words, then three first screens of the picked one. On 2026-11-01 three full directions each carried a trick and none landed; that is why only the first screen is drawn.
- **Never a one-pager (Tejjomay, 2026-11-08).** Every site we build is multi-page: a home plus real inner pages (about, work/catalog, service/product detail, contact, legal, etc). A single landing page or one long scroll is not a deliverable. Even a "simple" brief is split into pages.
- **Never basic, dead CTAs or plain boring components (Tejjomay, 2026-11-08).** No plain rectangular no-motion buttons, no undesigned links/cards/rows. The interaction layer (button, link, card, row, menu, cursor, reveals) is designed from the picked idea BEFORE sections are composed (see the reference code study in `reference-sites.md`). Do not make Tejjomay ask for basics. **The bar is the list in `high-ticket-flagship-3d/references/03-reference-sites.md`.** Print Elements and our own earlier builds are NOT the reference.
- Free fonts only (Google Fonts, Fontshare), licence recorded in tokens.json. Never lift a reference's commercial font, copy, images or code.
- Default motion feel: `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.35,1)` about 0.6-0.7s. "Feels dead" means change the curve, not the speed. What a button looks like and does comes from the idea; `components/Btn.astro` is only a working default.
- Demos are noindex and in their own Vercel project. Deploying is publishing: wait for the word.
- **Footer credit, hard rule:** exactly `Design & Systems · © Disojo Studio 2026` (HTML `Design &amp; Systems · © Disojo Studio 2026`) on the right of the footer bottom row, linking to https://disojostudio.com (as on Ascender Media, Print Elements, CSK Law NY). Never "Developed by Disojo Solutions". premium_gate.cjs fails a page without it.
- Test 1512x864 (plus 1440x790, 1920x1080, 1280x720) and phones 390/360/430 by measurement. Real phones and Safari are untested unless someone checks them: say so.
- Do only what was asked; finish a whole section, verify, then show with screens; say what is placeholder, stock, AI or untested.
