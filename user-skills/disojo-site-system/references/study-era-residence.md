# Study: ERA Residence (era-residence.com), by The First The Last

Tejjomay's stated favourite, 2026-11-10 ("i really fucking love it"). Walked with a real wheel at 1512 by 864: 37 stops down a 25,000 px page, plus phone first screen. Frames in `research/tftl-2026-11-10/era/`. Not yet walked: inner pages, apartment list, By Night state, hovers.

Study the thinking. Never lift its fonts, copy, images or code.

## Why it works
Everything on the page comes from the place. Nothing could move to another development unchanged.
- **Colour:** sky blue, bougainvillea magenta, plum, plaster cream. Taken from the view.
- **Bougainvillea:** seven transparent video clips that grow in from page corners and carry between sections. The local plant is the page's connective tissue.
- **The arch:** the loader opens through an arch into the photo, and arches return as section wipes. One shape, used for all the big transitions.
- **Day and night:** one click in the nav switches the whole site. The agency calls it a Day/Night storytelling system. It answers "what is it like to live here" without a sentence.
- **The coast line:** a drawn route between real towns shows the location.

## Mechanics measured
- Three voices only: a condensed high-contrast serif for display, a script for one accent word, a small extended sans for utility. Display reaches 366 px on "ARCHITECTURE".
- A full-bleed image and small framed images alternate. Largest image median 95% of the screen; 29 of 37 stops have an image over 40%.
- First screen is the name over one image with no subtext and no button block. The only ask is a small corner "Select an apartment".
- Same first screen holds on phone.
- Pacing: big image, a line of type, big image. Horizontal galleries, then an apartment list by type.

## Stack
Webflow, GSAP with ScrollTrigger and SplitText, Lenis, Barba, Lottie. No 3D files loaded. All of it can be done in Astro. Its display and script faces are paid; our free-font rule stands.

## What it needs that we must collect
Architectural renders in day and night versions of the same view, a cut-out plant or equivalent asset shot or rendered on transparency, brand identity done first. Most of its beauty is the renders. Without material at that level the method does not transfer.

## The agency
The First The Last, Dubai and Miami. 85 projects on its work page, tagged website 80, UX/UI 82, branding 44, motion 10. Three of Tejjomay's blind yeses were theirs: ERA Residence, Son Daven, Aventura Dental. Their range is the lesson: each client looks unlike the last.
