# Tejjo's eye on websites, in their own words

Why a site is beautiful or not, said by Tejjomay while scrolling the live site. Started 2026-11-10 before the Kiran Roy redo. This is the record my defaults lack: read it before writing the three ideas and before every first screen. Quotes are Tejjo's words (voice dictation, lightly cleaned). "Measured" lines are mine, from the live page.

## Ray Corral (raycorral.com), Person type, 2026-11-10
Sheet: `research/walk-2026-11-10/sheets/raycorral-com.jpg`

- "I like the font. The font is so clean. It's not a slop italic font."
- "The photo is really good. And the huge text, as well as the nav, is really good."
- "As we scroll down, the website isn't very overcrowded."
- "The image has a scroll wheel next to it. That's a nice design idea." (the press list beside the project photo)
- "The background is clean. It's not filled with any gradients or any slop."
- "The work section is so dope. It's like a full experience."
- "This just screams that it's not AI, it's genuinely designed really well."

Measured: one family only, Helvetica Now Display at 400, 500 and 700 (a paid font; we use a free one). The handwritten signature is the only other mark on the page.

What I take from it: one plain, heavy grotesk, no italic flourish. A real, strong portrait. Huge name. Clean flat ground. Few things per screen. Work shown as an experience, not a grid of cards.

## ERA Residence (era-residence.com), Product type, 2026-11-11
Said while scrolling the live site (voice, lightly cleaned):
- "A beautiful opening page. It opens in like a door sort of thing, and then that door opens up to the whole screen."
- "The font is beautiful. There is a beautiful image in the background."
- "A cute selector, by day, by night, which changes the image."
- "Hovering makes that up down effect."
- "View available apartments. Then it opens like a whole new oval thing from below."
- "It just scrolls beautifully, and everything is intentional and the fonts are beautiful."
- "The animations, the transitions, everything is just so well thought of."

What I take from it: the moments Tejjo names are moments where the page *does something from the subject*. A door (an arch, the building's own shape) opens into the place. Day and night is a real question a buyer has about a home. The oval rising is a window or a pool edge, not a UI modal. The fonts are named every time. Measured detail in `taste/era-residence.md`.
