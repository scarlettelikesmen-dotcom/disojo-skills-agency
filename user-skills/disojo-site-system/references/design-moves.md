# Design moves: what 84 sites actually do

Walked 2026-11-10: the eight sites Tejjomay approved and the yeses from their detail round. Real wheel scroll, hovers, menus, inner pages, phone, footer. Raw notes per site in `research/walk-2026-11-10/notes.md`, one contact sheet per site in `research/walk-2026-11-10/sheets/`, numbers in `research/walk-2026-11-10/data/`. Do not research these sites again. Open the sheet.

Organised by design, never by industry. A dentist can learn from a watch.

## Read this first: this file is not a menu

Everything here can be misused the same way the old hero formula was. Picking "word splits across the frame" and then finding a client to put it on is exactly how we made slop.

- **Order is fixed.** What only this client has, then the idea in words, then Tejjomay picks (`idea.md`). Only after that open this file, to push the picked idea further or to see how someone solved the same mechanical problem.
- **Never open this file to get an idea.** Never offer a move from it as one of the three ideas.
- **Swap test.** Take the client's name off. If the move still works for the next client unchanged, it is decoration. Cut it.
- **One or two moves per site.** The sites that work commit to one thing for the entire page. The sites that felt weakest stacked effects.
- **Never lift** a reference's font, copy, images or code.
- This file cannot make anything good. It can only stop us re-researching and give vocabulary. Tejjomay remains the check.

## The main finding

The best pages have one idea that came out of the thing itself, and the whole page is that idea.

| Site | The thing | What it became |
|---|---|---|
| Monolith | Heavy sculpted furniture | The wordmark's letters are cut like the furniture |
| Twofold Therapy | "Twofold" | Double-exposure photographs of one person |
| Minémal | The name | Splits into MIN and ÉMAL, each half explains itself |
| Teenage Engineering | Dry humour | Ten screens of following the product's absurd shadow |
| Daylight | A screen you can read in sun | The page goes from day to night |
| Grail | Timed quests | The real countdown clock is the section background |
| Gagunashvili Chess | The board | Tile system of crowns and knights frames everything; copy speaks in chess |
| Son Daven | Carpathian sheep and forest | Sheep and flowers in line-scan are the only illustration, and they dissolve as section changes |
| Palmo | Coconut shell | Scalloped edge is the only section change |
| ERA Residence | The coast | Bougainvillea from corners, arches, colours of the view, day and night |
| Ray Corral | Mosaic artist | Turning mosaic emblem, his signature closes the page |
| Work by W | "We work by hand" | One plaster hand stays on screen and gestures at everything |
| HISS | A school's years | Yearbook chapters 01, 02, 03 |

Compare the ordinary ones in the same list: Case, Kvadrat, Ekomia, Barry's, Muuto, Brené Brown, Terminal below the first screen. Clean, sensible, and nothing about them belongs to that client. Tejjomay said yes to their first screens. Do not take their page structure.

## First screen: six moves

For judging and pushing a picked idea only.

**1. The word is the image.** The name is so large or so drawn that nothing else is needed.
Monolith, Ever, The Line, Minémal, Halo, Squiggle, UND, Supersolid, Your Creative, Flim, Likova.
Needs: a name worth looking at, usually drawn specially. A free font set big is not this move. UND pins letters to the corners and lets them spread; Likova cuts the name into a white notch in the photo.
Fails when: the name is long or the letterforms are unremarkable.

**2. The subject breaks through the type.** Head, object or building overlaps the word, so type and picture are one thing.
Ray Corral, FS 60P, AIR, Steven Kotler, Poklonnaya 9, Son Daven, Aventura Dental, Halo.
Needs: a clean cut-out of a real subject, high resolution. Fails when: the cut-out is soft or the person is not the point of the business.

**3. One photograph and almost nothing else.** Everything rests on one image that could not be of anyone else.
111 West 57, Lingers, Kononenko, Terminal Industries, 1Million Dance, Flos, Twofold, OH Architecture.
Needs: an A-tier real photograph. Without it, do not attempt this move: say so at intake.

**4. Near silence.** Tiny type, huge space or sky. The restraint is the statement.
432 Park, Skyclinics, Mont Fort, Dishoom, ERA Residence, ZETR.
Needs: a client comfortable saying very little, and pages below that keep the discipline.

**5. Composed like a layout.** A poster or a magazine spread, not a hero with a picture.
UND, Work by W, Yale, Agrumea, LPAS, Realevate, Délice.
Yale is worth study: three photographs labelled Study, Explore, Engage, and the sentence uses those same three words. The sentence is the menu.

**6. A drawn or built world.** Illustration or objects replace photography.
Maxima, Santioni, HISS, Heron, Gagunashvili, Grail, Palmo, Son Daven.
Needs: real drawing or a real system. Most expensive to do well and the fastest to look like clip art (see Ascender v1 in `bottlenecks.md`).

Measured across the walk: median largest type 103.5 px, median 63.5 words per scroll step, median 54% of scroll steps have an image over 40% of the screen.

## Whole page: six structures

How the page holds together from top to bottom. Pick none in advance; the idea decides.

**A. One thing stays while everything changes.** Work by W's hand, Aevion's drone, FS 60P's watch, Palmo's coconut (cracks open and turns), Ciao's can, Likova's and Poklonnaya's towers, Ever's floating material spheres. Solves "what ties this page together" with one asset. Costs: that asset must be very good.

**B. One continuous place.** No sections at all, the camera just travels. Mont Fort flies from cloud to ship to Earth to leaves with no edge until the footer. Also Gufram (pan a field of objects, Space or Grid), Alche, Agrumea (scroll drives one carousel), Springs (home is one tilted wall of photographs). Costs: heavy production and hard on phones.

**C. Chapters by ground colour, from real material.** Ever's grounds are brick, clay, moss and sand. ERA's are the view. LPAS takes each colour from the building beside it. Girard-Perregaux moves gold to 70s red through its own history. UND has one per product line. HISS, Realevate, Monolog and Shortscut also change ground. Cheap and very strong, but the colours must be found, not chosen from a palette generator.

**D. Printed object.** 432 Park turns like a book, single photo then tiny centred text. Dishoom reads like a printed menu or newspaper. HISS is a yearbook. Works when the business has a real relationship with paper or time.

**E. A mark that runs through.** Squiggle's single drawn line wanders down the whole page. Gagunashvili's tiles. Son Daven's line fields. Maxima's paper creatures carrying across sections.

**F. The words do it.** Teenage Engineering's joke. Kasia Siwosz: STOP PLAYING SMALL turns into START PLAYING SMART in place as you scroll. Minémal. Costs almost nothing to build; needs an actual writer's idea.

## How one section hands over to the next

The biggest gap between these sites and our work. We stack sections; they hand over.

| Handover | Sites | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hard flip of ground colour | Monolog, Halo, Kasia, HISS | The cheapest good option; needs no effect |
| An edge shaped from the brand | Palmo scallop, Composites feather, Délice liquid, Grail blob, Terminal notch, Vaulk notch | Same shape every time. Shape must come from the client |
| Big arch or dome rises | ERA Residence, era.estate, Shortscut dome, Poklonnaya on phone | Only with a reason (ERA has actual arches) |
| Block rises over a held photo | Poklonnaya orange block, Silver Pinewood | Photo stays pinned for many steps, then something comes up over it |
| Window grows to full screen | era.estate, Likova notch shrinking, Lava dark window | A small frame becomes the next section |
| Photos travel past a held sentence | Lava, Silver Pinewood, Monolog ("WE CLOSE [image] THAT GAP") | Sentence pinned, images pass in front |
| The product's own behaviour | Daylight day to night, Teenage Engineering's shadow | Best when available. Rare |
| Slanted band | Spylt, Barry's | Reads cheap when alone |
| Cut-out moves across the seam | ERA bougainvillea, Maxima creatures, Poklonnaya feathers | An object sitting across two sections sews them together |
| Page returns to its start | Ciao, Monolith loop forever; Albert Holz and Belgrade Arbor end on their opening image | Good ending when there is nothing to sell at the bottom |

Pacing that recurs: a big image held for several wheel steps, one line of type, then big image again. 40 of 84 use sticky; only 7 use a GSAP pin that we could detect.

## What type does

- **Splits apart across the frame** as you scroll, the gap holding something: ERA Residence, era.estate, Aventura, Backhouse, Palmo.
- **Swaps in place:** Kasia.
- **Fills grey to black on scroll:** Kasia, Aevion.
- **Current item solid, neighbours ghosted** in a list: The First The Last, Aevion, Monolog's services.
- **Cropped by the frame edge:** Ever, Minémal's gold line, Monolog.
- **Image inside the sentence:** Shortscut, Monolog, Your Creative, Flim, Kozowood, Realevate.
- **Ghost giant behind photos:** Lingers, Backhouse, Grail's clock.
- **Wraps around a 3D object:** FS 60P.

Voices: two or three at most. Very often one display face plus one tiny label face. Stepped indents across lines (Minémal, Délice) stop centred type looking like a template.

## How work and proof are shown

Image first, name second, one or two numbers at most. Never a card with a paragraph.
- One case at a time, pinned: Shortscut. Name only over huge image: The First The Last. Uneven two-column drift with tiny captions: OH Architecture, Monolog, Kononenko. Tight bright mosaic: Supersolid, Light Factory.
- Numbers one at a time in the centre of a turning wheel of photographs: Kononenko. As a location story: Son Daven's "31 min".
- Press as a big list with hover images: Ray Corral. Press logos set large: Halo.
- Real people matter on every practice site: Lava's wall of actual smiles, Aventura's actual doctor, HISS's messy group shots.
- Apartments or products as drawn plans on pale tiles: Poklonnaya, Springs.
- A working tool beats a claim: Terminal's yard-cost calculator, Skyclinics' globe form.

## Hover and motion

Hover visibly changed something on 52 of 84 sites. Transition durations: 0.3s on 23, 0.4s on 18, 0.5s on 16, 0.2s on 15. Overall most used 0.3s (19), 0.5s (12), 0.4s (11), 0.2s (9). Easing: plain `ease` 28, a cubic-bezier starting 0.4 on 9, 0.25 on 8, `ease-out` 5, 0.16 on 4. Custom cursor on only 4.

So most are simple. Motion quality comes from what moves, not from unusual timing. House default stays `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.35,1)` 0.6 to 0.7s (`feedback-button-motion`).

Light Factory is the example of restraint with loud colour: neon appears only on hover, loader and page change.

## Menus

- Desktop toggle found on 14 of 84, otherwise a visible nav. Your Creative has a floating pill at the bottom on every size.
- Phone menu is a full screen almost everywhere. It is a designed screen: ERA's aubergine with script title, The Line's red with oversized links, Palmo's brown with drawn palm, Springs' with a yellow flower, Maxima's list of chapter names.
- About opens as a side drawer over the page on Monolog rather than a new page.

## Footers

- Full-width name on 7 of the 8 approved, usually with clock and place. Clock or time in the footer on 8 of 84. Median 28.5 footer words.
- With an idea in it: Halo's product as line drawing, HISS's changing portrait next to the name, Ray Corral's signature with live time, Mammoth's scrawl across the name, Flim's images in the letters, Son Daven's stacked sheep, Squiggle's name tangled with the line, Monolog's liquid shape, Work by W's mark assembling.
- Our own rule stays: real location, small live clock, the Disojo credit.

## Phones

No phone overflow on any of the 84. The better ones are rebuilt, not stacked: ERA and era.estate and Poklonnaya use arches and tall crops, FS 60P wraps type down the watch. LPAS shows the honest cheap version: same coloured tiles as a stack, and it works. Lingers shows what to avoid: a consent box taking half the phone.

## What it was built with

Globals we could detect, so bundled sites are undercounted: Smooth scroll 53. Video 42. Canvas 34. GSAP 18, ScrollTrigger 18, Webflow 17, Lenis 16, Nuxt 9, Barba 9, Swiper 8, Shopify 6, Lottie 5, Flip 5, Three 3, Next 2. Everything above can be done in Astro. Real 3D is rare and only on product (Tejjomay's rule: 3D is Product type only).

## Limits of this walk

Not properly seen: AIR (would not scroll for me), Agrumea (one section only), Kozowood's home (fluted hero never released), P448 (would not scroll), Bao (cookie gate), Sobha Privy (opening section only), Santioni (age gate, not answered without Tejjomay), Why Zero (must draw a zero to enter). Spylt was a teaser page. Composites' inner pages returned 404 to the walker. ERA Residence's inner pages and By Night were captured through the general walk only. Hovers were tested on what a script could find. Real phones and Safari were not used.
