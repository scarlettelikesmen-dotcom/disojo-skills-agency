# Direction families (vocabulary, not a menu)

**The idea comes first (`idea.md`).** These families are words for talking about how a page is built. Never start from one, and a screen that resembles a family's lineage has failed.

**Three, always (Tejjomay, 2026-11-08).** The three first screens carry the one picked idea in really different ways. Each must differ on at least three of: **type pairing, palette logic, layout skeleton, how the real thing moves.**

| # | Family | The principle | Lineage | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Editorial photo-led** | Their real photography carries the page; type is calm and large; numbered lists for work | OH Architecture; our CSK | Firms with good photos: architects, law, clinics, hospitality |
| F2 | **Loud flat colour** | One brand colour as the whole surface; condensed heavy caps; numbered service rows; real work photos framed tight | Mammoth Murals | Trades, makers, local services that want to be noticed |
| F3 | **Proof-in-the-headline** | A clean grotesk headline with real media set inside the words; stats as huge numerals; a light, product-like page | Shortscut; Monolog hero | Agencies, studios, software, anyone with strong results |
| F4 | **Wordmark as hero, dark** | The name at full viewport width on near-black with grain; work in uneven large tiles; a mono label system | Supersolid; Monolog's dark sections | Creative firms, confident brands with a strong name |
| F5 | **Kinetic word** | One word that changes (cycling, highlighted, giant) carries the positioning; accent used as a text highlight | SLIK | Brands with a sharp one-word promise |
| F6 | **Warm serif archive** | A literary serif at large size on a tinted paper; documents, years and artefacts as the structure; soft colour bands | HISS; our CSK and Postcards | Heritage, education, culture, premium consumer goods |
| F7 | **The system, drawn** | The business's process drawn as a diagram or object system that the scroll assembles; dark, technical, calm | Backhouse | B2B services, operations, production, logistics |
| F8 | **Illustrated and playful** | A drawn character or a hand-made line as the thread; rounded shapes; a friendly condensed display | Squiggle | Kids, education, food, community, consumer apps |
| F9 | **Studio split** | A small intro paragraph against a giant multi-line headline; light and dark sections alternate; proof cards in the hero | Monolog | Consultancies and service firms selling trust |

## Rules
- **Assets decide feasibility.** No good photos: F1 is out unless the brief lists a shoot. No proof numbers: F3 stats are out (say so). A weak name: F4 is out.
- **Never reuse a previous Disojo build's look** (memory `feedback-unique-design-per-client`). Name the nearest past build in the brief and say how this one differs.
- **Fonts must be free to use** (Google Fonts, Fontshare, or a licence the client holds). Record the licence. Never lift a reference's commercial font.

What each screen must state is in `templates/BRIEF.md`.
