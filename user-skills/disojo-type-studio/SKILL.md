# Studio

The buyer is deciding whether work like this could be theirs. So the site has to answer: what have you actually made, for whom, and how do I start.

Everything else comes from `disojo-site-system`. The look comes from the picked idea and nowhere else. If this file ever starts describing a layout, it has gone wrong.

## Where the idea usually hides
The studio's own working material, how a job really moves through the room, what they refuse to do, the name taken literally, the one thing every project of theirs has in common. Not a costume of their tools: Ascender's fake editor and timecodes were a metaphor about the craft and were rejected.

## Pages (minimum, never fewer)
1. Home
2. All work
3. One page per project, for every project with real material
4. What we do, in the client's words
5. About and people
6. Contact or start a project
7. Legal

## Proof that counts here
- The work first, the name second, one or two numbers at most. Never a card with a paragraph. All eight sites Tejjomay approved do this.
- Permission to show each project and each client name.
- Numbers only when verified, and who reported them.
- Named reviews with permission.

## Collect at signing
- [ ] Project list, in the order the client would be proudest of
- [ ] Highest-resolution images or video for each, with permission to show them
- [ ] Credits, year, one or two verified results per project
- [ ] Which clients may be named
- [ ] People: names, roles, portraits
- [ ] Vector logo
- [ ] How enquiries arrive today and who answers them

## Allowed tech
Astro, multi-page, every project from content files. No 3D without Tejjomay's words in `brief/IDEA.md`. Real footage only. Footer carries the real location and a small live clock, plus the Disojo credit.

## Benchmarks
Open only after Tejjomay has picked the idea: `disojo-site-system/references/design-moves.md`, sheets in `research/walk-2026-11-10/sheets/`.
Tejjomay's approved studios: Shortscut, Monolog, OH Architecture, Supersolid, Your Creative, The First The Last. Disojo's own site is on the approved list too; the walk found it carries more words and small boxes per screen than the others.
Also in the walk: The Line, Light Factory, Work by W, Realevate, Kononenko, LPAS, Mammoth Murals, Squiggle.

## Past failures on this type
- **Disojo redo, 2026-11-01:** three directions passed every check and none landed: 135-151 words a screen against about 50, and 0-2 big images in 8 screens against 3-8.
- **Ascender v1:** fake editor and timecodes decorating the page.

More in `disojo-site-system/references/bottlenecks.md`.
