Every site's moments (loader, page change, close) are designed new from that client's idea. The kernel's data-close settings, lab pages and defaults are mechanisms, never a look to reuse. The bar is TFTL's work, moment by moment.

**Why:** Tejjo, on seeing the four lab closes: "how can it be uniform, we have to have a new style for every client right, we cant be keeping a template, otherwise whats the point". Also: "all our websites have to be at par or beat tftl work, and this client is not a joke".

**How to apply:** in the idea step, each of the three ideas also names its opening, page change and close in words. Test: if the moment could sit unchanged on another client's site, it is not designed. Tune every kernel value from the idea.
