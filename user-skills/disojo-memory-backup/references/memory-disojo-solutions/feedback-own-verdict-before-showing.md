On 2026-11-11 (CSK fresh pass) I sent a first screen. Tejjo asked "Do you yourself think this is good?" and I said no. Then: "So why would you give it to me then if you yourself think it's not good."

**Why:** I treated finishing the steps (build, gate, side by side) as done and handed judgment to Tejjo. Sending work I do not believe in wastes their time and trust, the same failure as VÆL ([[feedback-gates-are-not-quality]]).

**How to apply:**
- Before sending any design, write my own verdict first: would I put it beside the benchmarks?
- If the answer is no, do not send it as a candidate. Say what is missing in one line (an idea, material such as a shoot, or craft), and either fix it or ask for what it needs.
- Only send when I can honestly say why it is good.
