Tejjo rejected the Remotion Postcards reel (stamp slams with camera shake, flash frames, cutouts on cream, text cards, phone mock, tray). Their words: "this doesn't look like a flagship insane case study, the shutter effects all that is lowkey unnecessary, think of more creativity and actual clean edits". Audio: Tejjo adds it themselves.
**Why:** it was a slideshow of assets with effects bolted on; no single visual idea carried it, and shakes/flashes read cheap.
**How to apply:** for reels, one visual idea from the brand carries the whole film (like Koto, ManvsMachine); clean cuts and match moves only, no shake, no flash frames, no gimmick transitions; full-bleed crafted imagery with depth, real holds. Propose the idea in words first ([[feedback-idea-before-pixels]]). Related: [[feedback-content-current-brand]].
