On 2026-11-11 Tejjo said "We have really got to do some research on our fonts... pick some better fonts". They disliked Marathon's Archivo + IBM Plex Mono and the first CTESCOA screens (Schibsted, Hanken, Big Shoulders, Public Sans, Martian/Space Mono). They pointed out that disojostudio.com (Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk) and Print Elements (Hanken Grotesk) use free fonts and look pretty, and said "don't copy those, but you know what's possible free too."

**Why:** I kept defaulting to safe Google grotesks. The benchmark sites almost all use paid foundry fonts, so free fonts have to be chosen and set with more care.

**How to apply:** follow the method in skill `disojo-site-system/references/free-type.md`. Draw from Google Fonts' 2024-26 additions, Fontshare, Collletttivo and Velvetyne. Set 15-20 candidates in the client's own words and send that specimen for a pick before drawing first screens. Never reuse Bricolage or Hanken for a client. CTESCOA specimen: `ctescoa-site/brief/type/`. Related: [[feedback-idea-before-pixels]].
