# Where assets come from, and what to check

- **Flat label artwork**: always from the client (dieline / print PDF). Never trace a photo of a label (the AI and the eye both mangle small print).
- **Generic pack models**: build from the kit's lathe profiles (free, ours), buy a model from a marketplace (Blender Market, Fab, CGTrader, Sketchfab store), or commission. Before using any downloaded model: read its licence (commercial use? redistribution inside a client site? attribution?). Two famous sites share one can file, which is exactly the situation to avoid for a distinctive brand.
- **HDR environments**: Poly Haven publishes CC0 HDRIs (check the page's licence line); use 1k for the web (about 1.4 MB). The kit defaults to three's RoomEnvironment (no download).
- **Textures for hand-made food/organic models**: photograph the real item; do not take textures from a stranger's site.
- **AI-generated models or footage**: record the tool and its terms in the project README; disclose AI use and SynthID (Gemini) to the client.
- Keep a `LICENCES.md` in every client project listing each third-party asset, its source and licence.
