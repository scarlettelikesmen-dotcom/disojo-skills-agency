# Packaging asset request (send to the client, or to their packaging designer, as one message)

Hi [name], to build the 3D product moments on your site we use a clean 3D model of your pack and wrap your real label artwork on it.
That is how the sharpest brand sites do it, and it means every flavour/variant stays pixel-accurate. We need the **print files, not photos of the pack**.

## A. Must have
1. **Label artwork as a flat print file** for each flavour/variant: the dieline / print-ready PDF, AI or layered PSD (outlined fonts, no crop marks cut off).
   If you only have a PNG/JPG, send the largest one and tell us it is not the print file.
2. **Pack type and real dimensions**: can (ml, slim/standard), jar (g/ml), bottle (ml), box/carton, pouch. Height, diameter (or width/depth) in mm.
3. **Label area**: where the label starts and stops on the pack (full wrap? band? how many mm from the base/top?). A dimensioned dieline answers this.
4. **Colours**: pack/cap/lid colour and finish (aluminium, matte, gloss, foil, soft-touch), plus brand colours as HEX or Pantone.
5. **What is inside and what shows**: clear glass or plastic? Liquid / product colour. Fill level.

## B. Strongly recommended
6. One **photo of the real pack** from the front and one from the side, in daylight (so we match cap, lid and material).
7. **Closure details**: lid type (twist, ring-pull tab, crown cap, pump), any embossing or shrink sleeve.
8. **Barcode / nutrition panel / legal text** artwork if it appears on the back (we keep the front hero label clean).
9. **Variants list**: exact flavour names and the order you want them shown.
10. Brand **fonts and logo** (vector).

## C. For us to confirm with you
- Do you want the pack to spin, tilt with scroll, or swap flavours with a spin (like a flavour carousel)?
- Is there anything the pack must NOT show (claims, certifications you do not hold)?

Reply with what you have; we will list what is still missing within a day.
