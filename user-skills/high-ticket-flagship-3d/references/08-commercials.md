# Commercials, positioning and risks

**Status of facts.** Working range: USD 10-15k (Tejjomay, 2026-10-14). Never quote a price or currency to a client without his say. Everything below marked "existing rule" comes from `~/Desktop/DISOJO SOLUTIONS/AGENTS.md`, an older document that uses rupee figures and disojo.in addresses; the principles are reused, the numbers are not. **Confirm each before relying on it.** Unresolved: public vs private pricing, payment schedule in USD, retainer in USD, timeline.

## 1. Positioning (how to describe the offer)
- It is not "a website". It is a brand and a site that look like a top studio made them, led by the founder, built around one concept.
- Proof is the work and the process, not claims. Do not use Postcards or Ascender as proof of quality for this tier. Until a flagship exists, the honest proof is a concept project and the method itself (`high-ticket-flagship-3d`'s gates).
- Tone to clients: calm, structured, specific. Never subservient ("Sorry for the delay", "Please let me know if the price is okay"). Address the client by name.
- Disojo's edge to state plainly: discovery first, stages the client can see, the founder leads every project, we stay after launch, and we tell you when something will not help.

## 2. What the 10-15k covers
Strategy, identity, 3D assets as scoped, the bespoke site, QA with numbers, handover, a 30-day check. The exact line items are in the signed scope sheet. If the budget is tight, **adjust the scope, never the rate** (existing rule: scope levers below).

## 3. Scope levers (what flexes instead of the price)
| Lever | Smaller | Larger |
|---|---|---|
| 3D hero objects | 1 | more objects |
| 3D variants | few | many (each needs label art or materials) |
| Scenes or chapters | 3 | 5 or more |
| Pages | Home plus Contact | Work pages, Process, About |
| Naming | client has a name | full naming round |
| Illustration or photography | client supplies | commissioned |
| Revisions | two rounds per stage | extra rounds are change orders |
| Editing (CMS) | none | added (a separate build item) |
No tiers are decided. If Tejjomay wants named tiers, build them from these levers and ask before publishing them.

## 4. Payment structure (existing rule, confirm for this tier)
For commercial clients the schedule is 20% before work starts, 20% at the first working milestone, 40% at the feature lock (80% collected before going live), 20% at handover. Map it to this engagement as: signature, Gate 2 or the first staged preview, the approved full preview, final handover. Final payment settles before IP release. Do not send a proforma or invoice until Tejjomay approves the amounts. Never put financial figures on public pages or in a public repo.

## 5. Ownership and release (existing rule, confirm)
No transfer of production repositories, cloud credentials, DNS or the IP deed until the final invoice is paid and reconciled. The client owns their brand identity and business content; Disojo keeps its reusable frameworks and tooling. Staging and demo access can be given during the build.

## 6. Revisions and change orders (existing rule, confirm)
Two consolidated revision rounds per stage. A revision adjusts something already in scope; it does not add a new feature or object. Feedback arrives as one numbered list. Anything new is a change order, quoted before work. The standing change-order rate in AGENTS.md is in rupees and does not apply to a USD project; ask Tejjomay for the rule.

## 7. Aftercare and retainer
The site promises "stay after launch". The 30-day check is in scope. A retainer is optional and unpriced for USD: ask Tejjomay before offering terms.

## 8. Risk register
| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 3D scope creep | The one unbounded item | Count objects, variants, scenes, moves in the scope sheet; extras are change orders |
| No model, no print files, no budget for an artist | Real 3D cannot be promised | Fit check at Phase 0; use `disojo-packaged-product-3d` routes; do not fake it |
| Late client material | Delays every gate | Asset request with dates; `TODO(client)` list; dates move with it |
| Single-person capacity | Identity, site and 3D is heavy for one lead | Decide early what Divyansh or a freelancer takes (3D, illustration); brief them with the no-copy law |
| Look-alike of a reference | Failed product, reputational risk | `04-no-copy-law.md`, distinctness test at every gate |
| Font and asset licensing | Legal exposure | Licence log; no trial fonts; no files from references |
| AI-generated media | Disclosure and quality | Disclose (SynthID mark); check every detail; never ship unchecked AI 3D or logos |
| Performance vs ambition | 3D pages are heavy | Weight budgets, fallbacks, measure on the deployed URL |
| Untested devices | Real phones and Safari are untested so far | State in every QA report; test on a real phone before launch |
| Client asks for a copy of a site | Conflicts with the law | Keep the intent, drop the copy (section 6 of the law) |
| Unverified claims in case studies | Credibility | Only claims the client or Tejjomay has confirmed, labelled as the client's |
| Quoting before deciding | Price and currency are unresolved | Ask Tejjomay first |
