# What makes a guidelines book good

Written 2026-11-22. The page list, format and handover live in `disojo-visual-identity/references/book.md` (built in code, exported to PDF, reviewed in the client portal). This file is the craft position.

## Position
A guidelines book has two jobs, and the template decks only do the second:
1. **Make the client fall in love with their brand again**, the first time they open it. Tejjo's reference reels ("can't stop staring at them") show this is the moment the client remembers.
2. **Let someone who was not in the room use the brand correctly**: their designer, their printer, their next hire.

## What separates ours from the floor
- **The layout is built from the identity's device.** The Trussly and Uprise decks share one template: title top left, small text top right, one panel. Ours: the crop marks, the ink, the arch, the grid of the brand itself run the pages.
- **It opens with the idea, in one sentence, and the strategy page**, before any logo. The why comes before the rules.
- **Real things, not stock billboards.** Their sign, their box, their screen, their uniform. Generated scenes are allowed (Tejjo, 2026-11-17) and disclosed; the stock billboard as the hero of every project is how the Andrija grid looks the same project after project.
- **Rules with reasons.** "Never stretch the mark" is obvious; "the accent appears only on the one action per screen, because it is the brand's voice saying 'here'" is a rule people keep.
- **Do and don't shown, not described.** The mark misused in six ways, each crossed out.
- **It is short enough to read.** 20 pages for Branding, as many as the brand world needs for the flagship. Fifty pages of filler is not more value; the reels brag about page counts, our clients should brag about the brand.

- **Never the Softriver page order** (Primary Logo, Logomark, Clearspace, Typeface, Hierarchy, Colour, Imagery, Patterns, Social, Mockups, Billboard, Merch). It is the template every client gets. Taken apart in `taste-library/studios.md`.

## Check before Tejjo sees it
- Every page screenshotted at full size and looked at.
- Read it as their junior designer: can I make an Instagram post and a business card from this alone?
- Would this book make a good flip-through reel for Disojo? If no page makes you stop, it is not done.
