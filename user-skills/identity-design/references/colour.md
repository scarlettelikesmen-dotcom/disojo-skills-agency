# Colour

Written 2026-11-22. Positions to argue, checked against what Tejjo loved: Level is black only on paper ("I'm so happy with the font and the branding"); Disojo is graphite; the amber and dark pairing on Level was used without a pick and rejected.

## Positions
- **Fewer colours, used with conviction.** Most strong identities are one main colour and neutrals. A palette of five equal colours is a template (the Trussly deck: red, pink, navy, grey, all at once).
- **Colour comes from the subject, named after it.** The oil in the Postcards bottle, the ink on Level's paper, the crop-mark cyan in print. A name like "Primary Blue" means nobody decided anything.
- **Black and white is a choice, not a lack of one.** If the mark and type carry the brand, no accent is often the strongest answer. Say so instead of adding a colour to look finished.
- **Proportion is the identity, not the swatch.** Write it down: for example 70% paper, 25% ink, 5% the accent, and where the accent is allowed (one action, one word, never backgrounds).
- **Never pick colour before the idea and the mark.** And never use a colour Tejjo has not picked in a build (Level 2026-11-17).

## The system to hand over
- Core: one or two colours that are the brand.
- Neutrals: the paper and the ink, with two or three steps between, tinted towards the core (pure grey looks dead next to a warm brand).
- Accent: optional, one, with a written rule for where it appears.
- Functional: success, warning, error for the website and portal, tuned to sit with the palette.
- For each: HEX, RGB, and for print CMYK and the nearest Pantone, checked on a real print when the brand is printed. Screen and print never match by default; say so to the client.
- Dark mode: designed, not inverted. Decide if the brand has one.

## Checks before showing
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 for body and 3:1 for large text (WCAG AA). A beautiful pale palette that fails this cannot be the website's text colour.
- The mark in one colour on each core colour and on a photo.
- The palette on their real things: the box, the sign, the screen. Colours shift on kraft, metal, fabric.
- Next to the 3 nearest competitors: if we match the category colour (every fintech blue, every wellness sage), say so and decide on purpose.

## What fails
- Gradients for their own sake, neon on black for "premium", pastel plus serif plus script (Postcards: "usual ai slop").
- A colour chosen from a feel word ("trust" so blue, "growth" so green). Feel is a finish, not the subject.
