# References by decision

Written 2026-11-22 from the studio study and Tejjo's ratings (`taste-library/studios.md`, scores in `taste-library/studios/ratings.json`). All paths below are under `~/.claude/skills/taste-library/studios/`. Contact sheets are in `_sheets/`.

**How to use this.** At each step, open the references for the decision you are making. Look at the images, then put our work next to them at the same size. A reference shows the **level** and the **why**. Never copy its look. Every client gets their own look (Tejjo's rule).

Tejjo's scores: 5 = most of it. Confirmed lower: TVG 2 and Kernal 3, both for being busy.

## Steps 5 to 8, the brand world (the finish Tejjo rates)
The surfaces that read as expensive, with where they are done well:
- Lightbox in a dark room: `andrija/graticule-02`, `andrija/shift-01`, `-07`
- Poster in a dark hall or car park: `andrija/perimeter-01`, `-08`, `andrija/graticule-06`, `-08`
- Poster on a real wall in daylight: `andrija/meridian-02`, `-03`, `andrija/sanora-a-01`, `andrija/orvexa-a-03`
- Packaging: `andrija/rooted-01`, `-03`, `-09`, `nonstop/smash-02`, `-07`
- Slipcase or book box: `andrija/meridian-06`, `-09`, `andrija/perimeter-06`
- ID badge on a lanyard: `andrija/perimeter-07`, `andrija/cortexa-03`, `andrija/shift-02`
- Business cards: `andrija/perimeter-09`, `andrija/sanora-a-03`, `andrija/meridian-08`
- App icon and home screen: `andrija/graticule-05`, `-07`
- The product inside the brand (screens, app): `nonstop/riverflow-02`, `-09`, `-12`, `nonstop/remono-12`, `-14`
- Merch (beanie, cap, tote, tee): `andrija/graticule-04`, `andrija/perimeter-02`, `-04`, `andrija/kernal-02`

**The rule for these surfaces.** The kit surfaces (lightbox, beanie, badge, car-park poster) are the finishing layer. Tejjo rates them 5, so ours must look at least this good. The hero surfaces come from the client's own world: their product, their shop, their screen. A brand world made only of kit surfaces is the template floor, however polished.
