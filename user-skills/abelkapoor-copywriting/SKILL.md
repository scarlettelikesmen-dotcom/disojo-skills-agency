# @abelkapoor copywriting (trading)

Read first: `content-hub/brands/abelkapoor-trading/` (strategy v3, wall-model-rules.md, voice.md, hook-bank.md) and `references/research-2026-10-18.md`. The full report is `content-hub/reports/trading-content-report-2026-10-18.md`.

## The one-line frame
**A profitable trader who teaches one simple model for free, so clearly that people wonder why there's no course.** Every line must make a viewer think "he actually knows this" or "he's being straight with me". Never "look at my money".

## What the research proved (do not re-argue)
1. **Two engines, both needed.** Reach comes from identity/story/relatable skits and list-style generosity (100K+ on tiny accounts: "'what do you do for a living' / 'I'm a funded day trader'" 105K at 1K followers; a retail-job origin story 117K at 556 followers). Trust comes from steady chart education in a named-model series (3-5K per reel, comments full of technical questions). Evan's biggest education reels did both at once: a generous list or a next-step with a concrete anchor ("YouTubers I watched to get 6 figures in payouts" 170K; "You just got your first payout, here's what to do next" 80K).
2. **Volume beats polish.** Evan: 207 reels in 56 days, median 9K, flat reach per reel; growth = volume + a few spikes.
3. **Captions are tiny.** "Save this video". The on-screen line does the work.
4. **Named model + catchphrase = brand.** "Asia ATM strikes again", "Wick Theory", "bottom ticker loosh", "TTP model". Ours: **The Wall Model** + the lines below.
5. **Honesty is a format.** Charlie posts break-even and stopped-out trades in the same headline format as wins; PB roasts his own mistakes. That is what makes the wins believable.
6. **Selling-heavy accounts plateau** (bonkanomics 3-7K with testimonials and DM keywords on every post). Lifestyle/car accounts get views but are banned for us (income implication).

## Signature language (use often, keep consistent)
- "Wall behind the stop. Road to the target." (the model in 7 words)
- "Two walls. No trade."
- "The Wall Model strikes again." / "The Wall held." / "No wall, no trade." (replay series titles)
- "Save this video." (caption)
- "Everything about the model is free." (trust line; never "I will never sell")
Coin new catchphrases only from Tejjomay's real speech.

## Script shapes (A talking head 25-40 s, ~60-90 words)
- **Rule** (Trust): hook -> the rule in one sentence -> why it works (one image: wall/road) -> the exception -> "Save this video."
- **Replay** (Trust): hook (3rd person or series title) -> "Here's what I saw: level, wall, road, trigger" -> the result as it happened (win, break-even or loss) -> one lesson.
- **Next-step** (Trust): "You just X" -> 3 numbered steps -> "Do the boring one first."
- **List** (Reach+Trust): "N things" -> N lines at one per 2-3 s on screen -> "Save this."
- **Story** (Reach): situation -> the turn -> what he does now -> no CTA (or "follow for the model").
- **Skit/POV** (B, Reach): 8-15 s, one line on screen, trending audio added in-app; no talking needed.
Voice from voice.md: short declaratives, second person, honest humility ("my strategy could still fail"), max one "isn't X, it's Y" per script, opinions marked as his ("I think"), no "most traders" statistics.

## Captions, CTAs, bio
- Caption: 0-6 words + 1-2 hashtags + disclaimer line. Default: "Save this video." Replay: "The Wall held." Story: no caption or one honest line.
- CTA ladder: month 1 "Save this video" / "Follow for the model". Month 2+: free Discord "Free Wall Model lessons in my bio". Month 5+: Psyche Society by keyword DM. Never two CTAs in one reel.
- Bio (draft, TO CONFIRM wording): "NQ & ES futures | The Wall Model | Free lessons below" + link. Never "verified trader", income, age flex.
- Pinned 3: (1) the whole model in one reel, (2) the real payout-email proof (labelled), (3) origin story.

## Compliance filter (run on every line; reject if any fails)
1. No win rate, no income projection, no "students made", no "copy my trades", no buy/sell now.
2. P&L/payout only as unedited proof of HIS result, labelled "Results vary. Not financial advice.", max ~1 in 10 posts, never as the hook of a teaching reel. (He approved P&L screenshots; brand.md line 14 still says otherwise: strategy.md v3 wins.)
3. "Education only. Not financial advice. Trading involves risk." in every caption; on-screen line kept inside the safe zone (y 360-1420).
4. No unsourced stats; no insulting named creators; ICT concepts credited to ICT, the packaging is his.
5. Facts about his life (age, job, story, money) ONLY from Tejjomay. Mark TO CONFIRM.

## QA gate before showing any copy
Hook <= 10 words and readable in 1 s? Concrete noun present? Matches an engine and a slot? In his voice (read aloud test)? Passes the compliance filter? Not repeating a hook used in the last 14 days? Then show with the formula number next to each line.
