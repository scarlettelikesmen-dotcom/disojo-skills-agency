# Voice profile: Tejjomay, trading / personal brand
Source: Whisper transcripts of 18 raw clips in ~/Desktop/PERSONAL BRAND REELS (days 2-7), saved in `brands/personal/_source/`. All English (p 0.76-0.98). Derived 2026-10-13; update as new reels arrive. Not Hinglish in this set.

## How the reels are built (the real pattern)
- One body script per day, filmed with 2-3 different **hook lines** (A/B/C). Same payoff, different first sentence. Keep doing this: it is a free hook test.
- Shape: hook line -> "Most traders don't lose because X. They lose because Y." -> a 3-beat mini story ("one loss, then another, then your account is blown") -> "The truth is simple." -> one-line payoff.
- Length 14-27s, ~45-70 words. One idea per reel.
- Recurring themes: execution over strategy, patience / doing nothing, risk per trade, overtrading, discipline.

## Voice
- Second person, direct: "you keep blowing accounts", "you're trading for dopamine".
- Short declaratives, plain words, no jargon. Says "we've all been there", "yeah, we both know", "the truth is simple".
- Takes a side but does not boast. Strongest trait: **honest humility** ("I might even lose money doing this", "My strategy could still fail", "backtesting is easy, executing is everything"). This is the differentiator in a niche full of P&L flexing. Lean into it.
- States his own rule as fact when it is true for him ("I risk only 0.5% per trade"). Only repeat such numbers when he confirms they are current.

## Hooks he has actually used (study the shape, do not recycle)
"This is why you keep blowing accounts." / "You're trading for dopamine, not for profit." / "You're not unlucky. You're just predictable." / "You don't need a better strategy. You need this instead." / "The problem isn't your setup. It's what you do after entering." / "Risk management isn't fun. That's why traders ignore it." / "My strategy could still fail."

## Watch-outs
- The "isn't X, it's Y" and "Most traders ... " structure is his signature, but the no-ai-slop skill flags it as a pattern. Resolution: keep it because it is his real voice, but at most once per script and never stacked with a second contrast.
- Generalisations about "most traders" are unsourced claims. Keep them as opinion ("I think"), not statistics.
- Never promise outcomes ("you'll be profitable"). Describe behaviour and process.
