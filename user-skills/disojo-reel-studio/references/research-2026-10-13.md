# Research: "20 repos to turn Opus 5.5 into your motion design studio" (beehiiv, 9 Oct 2026)
Read 2026-10-13. Cloned shallow into a scratch folder (nothing installed or executed); Remotion, Motion, GSAP, lottie-web are libraries and were not cloned.

## Adopted (already in the engine)
- **Barty-Bart/motion-graphics** (MIT; CC0 sfx): `sound-effects` kit (6 CC0 sounds + alternates, licence manifest, "never synthesise sounds in code", measure the exported MP4), `motion-broll` idea of full-frame cutaways timed to words and "never invent numbers". -> engine/sfx, panels.
- **nateherkai/hyperframes-student-kit** (license NOASSERTION: ideas only, nothing copied): `docs/EDITING-PLAYBOOK.md` house short-form style with numbers (split layout 30-42%, shots 1.1-1.4 s, boxed captions, soft SFX, bed 11 dB under voice, never invent numbers) and the "every note becomes a skill rule" loop.
- **whaleyxbt/claude-motion** (MIT): numeric motion rules, review-loop checklist (phone width, safe area, frame 0 not black), one timeline for picture and sound.
- **howseen-ai/claude-motion-design** (MIT): delivery checklist (BT.709 tv, -14 LUFS, 0 pops), critique loop (score /10, fix <= 7), damping >= 0.72, one thing moves at a time, adaptive motion-blur subframes (not yet used).
- **charlie947/motion-graphics-skills** (MIT): `reel-export` safe zone and upload-safe encode (H.264 High, BT.709 tv, faststart); `motion-effects` lessons (one object changing shape, ghost of the start state, the hold is where people look, label made-up numbers).
- **LottieFiles/motion-design-skill** (MIT): three motion layers (primary/secondary/ambient), personality archetypes, 1/3 rules.

## Available, not adopted yet
- **heygen-com/hyperframes** (Apache-2.0, 62k stars): HTML->MP4 framework with transitions registry (whip-pan, cinematic-zoom, flash-through-white), caption references, 21 skills. Our own seek(t) engine does the same job; mine its transition catalog before building whip transitions.
- **motion-broll engine** (spring "morph one shape" UI clips with cursor, 4-subframe motion blur): strongest next upgrade for explainer panels (a persistent shape that morphs between ideas). Landscape-first; would need a 1080x880 port.
- **product-launch-motion**, **hyperframes-motion-reel**, **animate**, **klik-anim/cam**, **iart motion-design-skills**: showreel/launch-film craft (camera grammar, beat-sync, logo animation). Useful if he wants a Disojo brand film, not for talking-head reels.
- **Remotion skills / claude-remotion-skill**: React video; caption components. Not needed.
- Student-kit **406 motion cards** and **style-library**: reference only (licence unclear).

## Not worth it for us
Beat-synced showreels, 3D title sequences, Vox-style explainers, AI-video B-roll pipelines (need paid generation). Music beds need a track he picks (MUSIC.md in the sound-effects skill lists free options; check each licence).
