# Software

The buyer is deciding whether this will really do the thing, and whether it is worth changing how they work. So the site has to answer: what changes for me, does it actually work, how do I try it.

Everything else comes from `disojo-site-system`. The look comes from the picked idea and nowhere else. If this file ever starts describing a layout, it has gone wrong.

## The rule, changed by Tejjomay's verdicts (2026-11-10)
This file's first draft said "show the real product screen first". Tejjomay rejected nearly every site that does: Linear, Cursor, Granola, Raycast, Mercury, Superhuman. The three software sites kept lead with something else: Grail with a game's world and a real countdown, Heron with a floor plan drawing itself, Terminal Industries with one big image.

So: **the first screen carries the idea. The working product is the proof further down.** Never open on a dashboard mock-up with a headline beside it.

## Where the idea usually hides
The before state the software ends, what its users actually make with it, a real number it counts, the one behaviour nothing else has, the domain's own drawings or objects.

## Pages (minimum, never fewer)
1. Home
2. How it works, with the real product
3. Who it is for or what it does, by real use
4. Pricing, only if the client wants it public
5. Customers, only real and permitted
6. Sign in and try or book, linking to the app
7. Security and legal

## Proof that counts here
- The real product, recorded or running. A working tool on the page beats a claim (Terminal's yard-cost calculator).
- Real user numbers, only when stated by the client.
- Customers named with permission.
- Never a fake dashboard, fake metrics or invented logos.

## Collect at signing
- [ ] Access to the real product. A test account the client creates; they log in themselves, we never type credentials
- [ ] Screen recordings of the real flows, or a time to record them
- [ ] Real numbers and who verified them
- [ ] Customers who may be named, with permission
- [ ] Pricing, or the decision not to show it
- [ ] Sign-in, sign-up and billing URLs. Link to them, never rebuild them
- [ ] Security or compliance statements, as the client's own documents
- [ ] Vector logo and app icon

## Allowed tech
The marketing site is Astro, multi-page. The app is outside the Astro rule. No 3D without Tejjomay's words in `brief/IDEA.md`. Footer carries the real location and a small live clock, plus the Disojo credit.
