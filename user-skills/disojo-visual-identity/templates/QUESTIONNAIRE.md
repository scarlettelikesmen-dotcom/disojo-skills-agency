# Questionnaire: CLIENT NAME

Sent at step 2, before the workshop or call. Plain questions, answered in their own words. Short answers are fine; voice notes are fine.

## The business
1. What do you do, in one or two sentences, the way you would say it to a friend?
2. Who buys from you today? Who do you wish bought from you?
3. What is true today: how long running, where, how many people, what you are known for. Only what you are happy for us to say in public.
4. What makes people pick you over the nearest alternative? Who is that alternative?
5. Where do new customers come from today, and how do they get in touch?

## The brand today
6. What do you have now (name, logo, site, social)? What must stay, what can go?
7. Where does your business look smaller than it is?
8. Is the name fixed?

## Where you want it
9. Three words for how it should feel.
10. Brands you love, from any category, and why. Brands you never want to look like.
11. Words, colours or styles you never want used.
12. Anything your trade or regulator requires us to say or not say.

## The practical side
13. Who decides, and who approves day to day?
14. What material exists: photos, videos, product shots, reviews you have permission to use?
15. Any dates the launch has to meet?
16. Monthly revenue band: under $20k, $20k to $40k, $40k to $100k, $100k and up.
