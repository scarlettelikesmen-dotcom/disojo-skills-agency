# Strategy: the workshop and the one page

Proven once, on Level (2026-11-17, Tejjo as the founder, 3 rounds, "yes lets go"). Files to copy the shape from: `~/Desktop/DISOJO SOLUTIONS/level/strategy/interview.md` and `summary.md`.

## The workshop, run as rounds
- Start from the questionnaire answers. Ask only what they did not answer, a few questions at a time.
- Write their answers down in their words, lightly cleaned. Facts only. My reading goes under "My notes", marked as mine.
- After each round, list what is still not answered. Stop when the one page can be written without guessing.
- Flagship: a live workshop (call or in person), length `___`, recorded with their OK and transcribed. Branding: one call.

What each round must reach:
1. What it is, who it is for, the problem in their words, what is true today (not planned), what is fixed (name, colours they will not lose).
2. The first person the brand speaks to (not "everyone"), an honest promise true on day one, the feel in their words, voice, the one job of the website.
3. Brands they love and hate in their category and outside it (open each one and study it), words they never want used, anything regulated.

## The one page (`strategy/summary.md`)
Sections, in this order: what it is · who the brand speaks to · the problem, in their words · why them and not the nearest competitor · the promise (true on day one) · the one-line idea (mine) · what we can say is true today · voice · feel (a finish, not the subject) · what the name already holds (raw material, not ideas yet) · the website's one job.

Rules:
- A claim they cannot prove today does not go in (Level: "become profitable" became "a plan you actually follow").
- The feel word is a finish. Glass, calm, premium: how it is made, never what it is about.
- The client signs this page off before step 4. If they change it later, the idea is checked again.
