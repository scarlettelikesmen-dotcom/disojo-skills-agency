# The guidelines book and the brand world

Untested: Disojo has not made one yet. The first book made under this file sets the standard; log Tejjo's verdict in `taste-library/identity.md`.

## Why ours is different from the floor
The Softriver (Trussly) and Nino (Uprise) decks share the same page list and the same layout: title top left, small text top right, one image panel. That is a template. Ours is laid out from the identity's own device (Print Elements would be built on crop marks; Level on the ink and the paper). Look at the reference reels before starting so we know what to beat (memory `reference-branding-flagship-refs`).

## Pages (the content floor; the layout is ours)
Branding, about 20 pages:
- Cover, the idea in one line, the strategy in short
- The mark: primary, secondary, symbol, construction, clear space, minimum size, what not to do
- Colour: names from the subject, values (HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone if printed), proportions
- Type: faces, licences, hierarchy set in the client's own words
- The device: how the identity repeats it
- On their 3 real things
- Files and contacts

Flagship adds:
- Voice: how it sounds, words used and never used, before-and-after lines in their words
- Imagery: photo or render direction, what is real, stock or AI
- Motion: how the mark and the page move (curve `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.35,1)`, timing)
- Graphic system: patterns, grid, icons if needed
- Digital: website, social templates, profile, email signature
- The brand world: signage, packaging, merch, print, whatever the business really uses

## Making it
- SETTLED 2026-11-22 (Tejjo): the book is built in code (HTML pages from `brand/tokens.json`), exported to PDF, and delivered in the client portal for review and approval. Every page screenshotted and looked at before Tejjo sees it.
- Mockups: real photos of their things where possible; generated scenes are fine (Tejjo, 2026-11-17) but disclosed. No stock billboard as the hero of the book.
- Hand the source over with the PDF.

## Final files (handover)
Logo in SVG, PDF, PNG (colour, ink, white; transparent), favicon set, the book PDF and source, fonts or links with licences, colour file, templates. Folder named and ordered so the client can find things without us.
