# Presence kit and launch content (flagship only)

Untested as a package. The list is SETTLED in `flagship-offer.md` (2026-11-22); the $20k extras are proposed there.

## The kit
- Instagram: profile picture (reads at 40 px), bio in the brand voice, highlight covers, templates for post, carousel and reel cover.
- LinkedIn: banner and company page text.
- Google Business profile: photos and description.
- Email signature, favicon, link-share images (from the site).
- Motion logo (short, on the Disojo curve).
- A pitch or sales deck template.

## Launch content: a 9-post grid plus 3 reels (SETTLED 2026-11-22)
- Planned as one grid: the 9 posts read as a whole on the profile.
- Built with `disojo-carousel-studio` and `disojo-reel-studio`, in the client's brand, never Disojo's look.
- Copy from the strategy page and the client's own words. No invented numbers, reviews or claims.
- Footage: what the client has or films. If they have nothing usable, say so at signing and agree a shoot or a different reel idea.
- We make it; the client posts it. We never log in to their accounts.

## Not included
Running their accounts, posting, community management, ads. That is a content agency, which Disojo is not (2026-11-09).
