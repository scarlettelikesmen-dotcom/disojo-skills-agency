# @disojo.solutions copywriting (studio, client leads)

Read first: `content-hub/brands/disojo/` (brand.md, voice.md, strategy), the verified-claims list in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, and `references/research-2026-10-18.md`. Full report: `content-hub/reports/disojo-content-report-2026-10-18.md`. Editing style is locked in `disojo-editing-locked`.

## The one-line frame
**We find where an owner-run business leaks time and money, then build the software that plugs it, in stages they can see.** Talk to the OWNER about THEIR business, never to other freelancers about agency life.

## What the research proved
1. **Almost all "automation" content sells the dream to would-be agency owners** (n8n templates, "I sell this for $400/month"). The owner-facing lane is nearly empty. That is our lane.
2. **Owner-POV result stories win**: "ChatGPT is recommending my vehicle wrap shop... I set it up with one file" 59K likes / 14K comments. One business, one place, one mechanism, one result.
3. **"Boring" is a trust word**: "6 boring automations" 4K / 2.3K.
4. **Show the work moving and say how it feels**: "A coffee website that feels warm before you even visit" 31K; "Immersive beauty cream website" 70K.
5. **First-person studio positioning beats service lists**: "I'm a web designer... I prefer to do everything custom" 47K vs "Launch your business online for just 1,200!" 580.
6. **Owner debates pull owners into the comments**: "Cheap websites or premium websites?" 43K.
7. **Promo creatives and price ads die.** No prices in public anyway.

## Owner language (use their words, not ours)
Say: "texts and DMs for bookings", "the spreadsheet only one person understands", "retyping the same details", "chasing payments", "is 5 pm free?", "the reschedule", "the thing only you know how to do", "hours back every week", "it just works", "stays working after launch". Avoid: "digital transformation", "solutions", "leverage", "synergy", "cutting-edge", and India-specific terms (WhatsApp, GST, UPI, rupees). Bombay as honest context is fine.

## Signature series
- **"Where the work leaks"**: one leak per episode (bookings, payments, retyping, tribal knowledge) with the fix we'd build.
- **"If you run a ___"**: academies, studios, clinics, trades, D2C brands (only examples we can stand behind).
- **"Built for [client]"**: case studies from the verified list (KDC, Postcards, Ascender, Sudha Mahesh Thakkar, Mentable reviews), numbers exactly as verified, client-reported where it is.
- **"Friday build notes"**: what clients see each week (staging links, not secrets).

## Script shapes (A 25-40 s; locked Disojo edit)
- **Leak story**: the owner's pain in their words -> what it cost (time, missed bookings) -> what we built (show it) -> the result (verified number or "they stopped doing X") -> "Where does your business leak?"
- **Showcase**: the site moving, 0-2 s -> how it feels (one sensory line) -> one detail that took craft -> who it's for.
- **Method**: the question/stage -> why it matters to an owner -> what goes wrong without it.
- **Take**: founder line -> one example -> back to the line.

## CTA ladder
Month 1: "Follow for one leak a week." Month 2+: "Comment LEAKS" (free 7-question leak check, TO CONFIRM wording) -> DM -> discovery call (cal.com link in bio). Never a price. Max one CTA per post; sales-y posts max 1 in 10.
Bio (draft): "Software for owner-run businesses | We find where the work leaks | Book a discovery call" + disojostudio.com.

## Claims filter (reject if any fails)
1. Numbers only from the verified list (50+ projects, Ascender +70% local inquiries and 100/100, Postcards ad return 0.6x to 3.7x and +42% bundles as client-reported, KDC 10 hrs back weekly). Anything else: TO CONFIRM.
2. Client names only from the OK'd list; Nature's Blend: never mention WhatsApp.
3. No prices, no currency, no discounts.
4. No India-specific references; no competitor insults by name (blunt opinion lines are Tejjomay's call, flag them).
5. Calm, sovereign voice: never "please", "sorry", "we'll do anything".

## QA gate
Speaks to an owner (not a freelancer)? Owner words, concrete pain? Hook <= 10 words? Claim on the verified list? One CTA? Fits the locked edit (panels with real screenshots)? Then show with formula numbers.
