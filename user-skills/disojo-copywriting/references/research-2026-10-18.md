## Patterns (Disojo)
1. Show the work moving, describe how it FEELS ("feels warm before you even visit"). 2. First-person studio positioning beats "we offer X, Y, Z". 3. Owner-facing debates (cheap vs premium) pull owners into comments. 4. Owner-POV result stories (vehicle wrap shop + ChatGPT) are the strongest owner content. 5. "Boring" is a trust word. 6. Promo/price creatives die. 7. Most automation content targets freelancers: the owner-facing lane is open.
