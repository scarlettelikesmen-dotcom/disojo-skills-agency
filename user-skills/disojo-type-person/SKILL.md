# Person

The buyer is deciding whether to give this one person their attention or money. So the site has to answer: who is this really, what do they actually believe, what can I get from them.

Everything else comes from `disojo-site-system`. The look comes from the picked idea and nowhere else. If this file ever starts describing a layout, it has gone wrong.

## Where the idea usually hides
The person's craft in their own hands, a sentence only they would say, their signature or handwriting, an object they keep coming back to, the real turning point in their story. Never the generic creator page.

## Pages (minimum, never fewer)
1. Home
2. Their story, in their words
3. The work (books, talks, art, music, courses), with a page per major piece that has real content
4. How to work with them or buy, linking to whatever really takes the money
5. Press or speaking, only when real
6. Contact
7. Legal

## Proof that counts here
- Real portraits. At least one strong enough to carry a whole screen, or say so at intake.
- Their own words, unedited in meaning.
- Press, students and results only when real and permitted. Any income or results claim needs its source and may be regulated: ask, never guess.

## Collect at signing
- [ ] Portraits: count them and grade them now. Casual phone photos cannot carry this type. Book a shoot if needed
- [ ] Their story in their own words: a recording is better than a brief
- [ ] Everything they sell, with live checkout and login links. Link to these, never rebuild them
- [ ] Press, logos, quotes, each with permission
- [ ] Signature or handwriting, if they have one worth using
- [ ] Which catalogue titles they are willing to see on the homepage
- [ ] Vector logo, if any

## Allowed tech
Astro, multi-page. Checkout, login and course platforms stay where they are. No 3D without Tejjomay's words in `brief/IDEA.md`. Footer carries the real location and a small live clock, plus the Disojo credit.
