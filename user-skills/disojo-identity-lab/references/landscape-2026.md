# What strong identity work looks like right now (research 2026-11-02)

Sources: It's Nice That "graphic trends for 2026" (Jan 2026), It's Nice That on the BBH × Studio Drama rebrand (Feb 2026), Elizabeth Goodspeed on studios making fonts (Oct 2025), Behance most-appreciated branding and logofolios 2025–26 (contact sheets in `shots/`). Brand New's best-of-2025 is paywalled, not read.

## The big shifts
- **Type is the hero asset.** Studios draw their own letters (Koto: Faculty Glyphic; BBH: three Studio Drama typefaces; Order, &Walsh and R&M run foundries). R&M's Triad gets its triangular counters from the site's footprint: the letterforms carry the concept. A wordmark with *several* redrawn glyphs and its own logic beats a font plus a symbol.
- **The mark is a family of objects, not one icon.** BBH's "zag" is sketched flat and then made in CGI as dozens of glossy objects (a melted trolley, a fried egg, a furry spiral, a metallic zigzag). Bloom and Nonstop (Tejjo's refs) do the same: flat, then 3D chrome, then grid pattern. **Test: can the mark live as a physical object and in motion? If not, it is too thin.**
- **Two directions only.** 2025 identities went either all-in maximal expression, or rigorously minimal *with a twist* (Creative Pool 2025). The middle (tasteful, flat, polite) is invisible. Rounds 1–4 lived in the middle.

## Named 2026 trends (It's Nice That) and how they show up in marks
1. **Blotch:** melting, leaking or blurred letterforms and marks, variable and organic (Skims, Mud, Wolke's logo that melts at the base, Chantelle's shape-shifter). The Behance top lists show Nth (a blur bloom behind a heavy N) and MOMA HOUSE (a heavy grotesque with a blurred cobalt script M behind).
2. **Pick and mix:** patchwork or ransom lettersets, scavenged letters, tape lines, handwriting fonts (Design Office Fun's Trobar logo built from letters found in bookshops).
3. **Micrographics:** tiny technical type, grids, coordinates, timestamps and contract layouts used as texture (Astrae, Uncommon).
4. **Make me a copy:** photocopy, scanner and Risograph artefacts; a logo that fades as the ink runs out.
5. **Visual index:** cut-out objects laid out like specimens.

## What wins on Behance 2025–26 (from the contact sheets)
- **A gesture over a grid:** a big single-line script drawn huge and cropped over technical type (Trato, Youni, Ralo, The Pair). Caps plus script overlay is everywhere: the same tension Tejjo loved.
- **Character and energy marks:** figures and creatures with drawn energy (Nabiji's figure, a Kiketi-style beast, a dog-monster, a trident "3", a chrome swirl G). Not cute mascots: graphic, punchy, almost tattoo-flash.
- **Neo-tribal and chrome symbols:** sharp, symmetrical, Y2K liquid-metal glyphs.
- **Swollen drawn wordmarks:** puffy, heavy, hand-drawn letters (Orange Pottery).
- **Heavy speed italics** with a small speed glyph (Studio Set).
- **Always shown in a world:** embroidered on a jacket back, stamped on bread bags, blown up and cropped on a wall, on a cake. The presentation is half the persuasion.

## For a software studio specifically
- Software, AI and fintech have no physical product, so type and motion carry the whole brand (Goodspeed). A studio's identity must survive at 16 px in a tab **and** be strong enough for a 3D hero and a merch drop.
- Avoid the SaaS default (geometric sans, gradient blob, a rounded-square glyph). Every agency in Bombay and every YC startup looks like that.
