# Territories: visual worlds to explore (pick 6–8 per round, never all from one row)

Each one is a construction, a tool and an attitude. "Fits" says what kind of idea it carries well. Add new ones after research.

| # | Territory | What it looks like | Tool / world for WORLD prompt | Fits | Drifts into (avoid) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Blotch / leak** | One letter melts, drips or bleeds while the rest stays solid; or a blurred bloom behind a heavy word | liquid chrome, wet ink, glossy resin | a story about something going wrong and being caught | slime, horror drips, gradients |
| 2 | **Eyes in the letters** | O's or counters become eyes with off-centre pupils, glancing | hoodie print, sticker, animation | "we look first", any name with o/ojo/oo | cartoon faces, eyelashes, emoji |
| 3 | **Patchwork / pick and mix** | Every letter from a different style, locked to one height and weight | riso posters, wheat-paste | joining mismatched things into one system | ransom-note chaos, illegible |
| 4 | **One line / tube** | The whole word as one continuous bent tube or line | chrome pipe, neon tube, bent wire | continuity, "typed once", one system | cursive signature, infinity sign |
| 5 | **Sigil / neo-tribal chrome** | Sharp, symmetrical crest built from the letters, mirrored | liquid-metal CGI on black, tattoo flash | palindromes and symmetric names, attitude | flames, wings, skulls, metal-band clichés |
| 6 | **Speed italic** | Ultra-heavy forward italic, ink traps, a speed slit, racing-number ovals | livery decal, team jacket | "it runs", momentum | checkered flags, swooshes |
| 7 | **Swollen / inflated** | Puffy, heavy, hand-drawn letters, tiny counters, bounce | inflatable vinyl, sticker sheet | approachable, human, owner-facing | children's toy, balloon party |
| 8 | **Character / flash** | Graphic figure or creature, thick strokes, tattoo-flash energy | rubber stamp, embroidered patch | a hero action (the fixer, the guard) | mascot cuteness, clip-art |
| 9 | **Gesture over grid** | Huge single-line script swept over technical caps or micro-type | jacket back, giant crop on a wall | founder's hand + system | brush calligraphy (banned for Tejjo), the old Disojo loop |
| 10 | **Micrographic label** | Wordmark plus tiny specs, coordinates, version numbers as texture | product label, hardware sticker | engineering, precision | fake data, unverified claims (never invent numbers) |
| 11 | **Overprint / misregistration** | Two ink layers offset, a darker overlap | screen print, riso | print, layering, collaboration | glitch-art cliché |
| 12 | **Object first (BBH zag)** | Design the mark as a 3D object family first, derive the flat later | CGI objects in different materials | brands that need a hero visual world | random 3D without one shape idea |
| 13 | **Copy / scan** | Photocopy fade, scanner drag, low ink | photocopied flyer | rawness, anti-corporate | dirty for its own sake |
| 14 | **Constructed caps (Monolog type)** | Heavy custom caps with several redrawn glyphs and one consistent logic (a cut, a counter shape) | large flat poster, signage | type-led brands | font + one gimmick (Tejjo: "disgusting") |
