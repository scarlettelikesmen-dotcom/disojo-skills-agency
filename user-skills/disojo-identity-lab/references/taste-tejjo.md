## Post-mortem, rounds 1–4 (2026-11-02)
1. **One render world for 40 prompts.** Every prompt ended in "flat vector on warm off-white, near-black plus one hex accent, no gradient, no shadow". So the ideas changed but every output looked like the same polite icon set. They judge the *image*, not the idea.
2. **Meanings, not forms.** "It means we look first" can't be drawn. The model fell back on stock icons (a window, an arch, a table) that read as an icon library.
3. **Literal metaphors** of the business (keystone = holds, knot = holds, seam = joined) look like clip-art, and the skill already banned that.
4. **No energy, no voice, no tension.** The marks in the 2026 logofolios have attitude: drawn energy, character, chrome, speed, melt. Mine were Bauhaus homework.
5. **No world.** The refs show marks on jackets, objects, chrome renders and motion. Mine showed marks floating on paper, so nobody could feel the brand.
6. **I asked the image model for exact geometry** (perfect circles, 4x4 grids), which is its weakest skill and my strongest. The split of work was backwards.
7. **I never put my output next to a real reference** before sending (the rule from 2026-10-21, ignored).
