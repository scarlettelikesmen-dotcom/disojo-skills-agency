# Asset request (send this to the client as-is, then ask them to reply in one thread or one shared folder)

Hi [name], to build your site we need the items below. The first group is what we cannot start without; everything else makes it better.
Send originals, not screenshots or WhatsApp-compressed copies (on WhatsApp use "Document", or share a Drive / Dropbox / WeTransfer folder).

## A. Must have (we cannot start without these)
1. **Product list**: every product's exact name, size, price, and a 1-2 sentence description in your own words.
2. **Product photos**: for each product, one clean photo straight on, label fully readable, plain background if you have it.
   Phone photos are fine in daylight (no flash). Landscape or portrait, at least 2000 px on the long side.
3. **Logo**: vector (SVG/AI/PDF) if you have it, otherwise the largest PNG.
4. **Contact details to show on the site**: email, phone/WhatsApp, Instagram handle, address if you have a shop.
5. **How people buy**: do you use Shopify / WooCommerce / WhatsApp orders / something else? Share the store link.

## B. Strongly recommended (these make it look premium)
6. **Label wording, exactly**: a photo of the real label, or the text typed out (notes, ingredients, size line). We copy it letter for letter.
7. **A short video of the product turning** (5-10 s, steady camera, plain background), or tell us and we will create one.
8. **Brand colours and fonts** if you have a brand kit (otherwise we propose a palette).
9. **Your story**: how it started and why you make this, in your own words (voice note is fine; we will transcribe).
10. **Real customer reviews** that we may publish (screenshot + permission is fine). We will not invent any.

## C. Business details we need to be accurate
11. Shipping areas and times, returns policy, payment methods.
12. Any offers or bundles (exact prices and what is included).
13. Wholesale / corporate gifting: do you offer it? On what terms?
14. Anything we must NOT claim (medical, "best", certifications you do not hold).

## D. Reference
15. Two or three websites you like and what you like about them.
16. Your current website or store link and what you want changed.

Reply with whatever you have today; we will list what is still missing within a day.
