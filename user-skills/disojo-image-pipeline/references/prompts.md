# Gemini prompt bank (mood images, tier C)

Structure: **medium and film** + **subject doing a real thing** + **light** + **lens and depth** + **mood** + **format** + **exclusions**.
Always end with: `no text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks`. Add `no faces` unless a face is the point.
Generate 4, check at 100% (hands, fingers, tool tips, straight lines, reflections), grade with the site look (`grade.py`).
Never place these next to a client name, result or quote.

## Process / "we learn the business first"
- Black and white photograph, 35mm film grain. A notebook open on a wooden desk beside a cup of coffee, a hand mid-sentence writing with a pencil, soft morning window light from the left, shallow depth of field, calm, unhurried. Landscape 16:10. No readable text on the page, no logos, no faces.
- Documentary photograph, muted colour, film grain. Two people at a small shop counter looking at a paper ledger together, shot from behind one shoulder, warm practical light, background softly out of focus. Landscape 3:2. No faces visible, no text, no logos.

## Building / "you see it as it is built"
- Black and white macro photograph, film grain. Hands assembling a small wooden architectural model on a cutting mat, steel ruler and scalpel nearby, single soft light, deep shadows. Landscape 16:10. No text, no logos.

- Black and white photograph, film grain. A piano tuner's hands on the strings inside an open grand piano, tuning lever in hand, soft overhead light, shallow depth of field. Landscape 16:10. No text, no logos, no faces.
