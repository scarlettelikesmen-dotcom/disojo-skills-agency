I think the loneliest stage of life is when you know you're changing but nothing around you has changed yet. You know you're changing but you still have the same room, same people in your life, same routine as ever. But mentally you were send it to a different level. You start caring about different things, you start prioritizing your work over everything else and most importantly you start thinking in a separate way. And nobody around you fully understands what's going in your head. Not your parents, not your friends and not your partner. That stage is really weird because while externally you may look the same, internally you're a whole different person.
