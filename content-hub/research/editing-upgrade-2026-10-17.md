# Editing upgrade research (2026-10-17). Trigger: Tejjomay said the current editing is "very mid: no transitions, no graphics, no motion". Matters hugely for @disojo.solutions and @codewithabel; trading stays restrained.
## Honest diagnosis of engine v4
Strong: retake-free cuts, HDR->SDR, branded captions, panels, CC0 SFX. Missing: transitions between segments (hard cuts only), b-roll/cutaways, motion graphics beyond panel cards, screen-recording treatment (zoom/cursor), code/terminal animation, device mockups, camera moves beyond a slow push, transition sound design. Architecture limit: overlays are rendered as PNG sequences from Chrome and composited by ffmpeg, so video and graphics never live in one scene.
## What exists (we already have it, not using it)
- ffmpeg here has `xfade` with 57 transitions (fade, wipes, slides, circle, zoomin, squeeze, wind, cover/reveal, pixelize, hblur...). Never used in the engine.
- Skills not yet used for video: motion-foundations/patterns/advanced (spring tokens, React patterns), review-animations (Emil Kowalski audit bar), emil-design-eng, ui-demo (Playwright screen recordings with injected cursor = known cursor coordinates for auto-zoom), disojo-scroll-3d-site-recipes + 3D lookdev (3D hero scenes could become reel b-roll), frontend-slides animation patterns, high-end-visual-design, anti-slop-design.
## Strong candidate: Remotion (React -> video)
- Renders React compositions in headless Chrome: our current approach, but with the video, text, springs, transitions and graphics in ONE scene (no PNG-sequence overlays).
- Official: `@remotion/transitions` (slide, wipe, fade, flip, clock-wipe...), `@remotion/captions` + `createTikTokStyleCaptions()` and a TikTok-captions template (Whisper transcription), `@remotion/three` (3D), `@remotion/lottie`, motion blur, noise, shapes, Google fonts. Official agent skills: `npx skills add remotion-dev/skills` (docs: remotion.dev/docs/ai/skills). Community: remotion-captions-themes (word-level kinetic caption themes; check its licence).
- Licence: free for individuals and organisations of up to 3 people (commercial use included); a paid company licence above that. Disojo is solo: fine today. Fonts/music/footage carry their own licences.
- Caveat: renders via a headless browser (slower on heavy SVG/canvas); iPhone HDR clips must still be converted to SDR first (reuse our sdr_chain), then fed in.
- Alternatives considered: Motion Canvas (editor-first, canvas only), Revideo (Motion Canvas fork with headless API; roadmap tied to a commercial product per one source). Remotion wins on React/DOM/ecosystem/batch rendering.
## Screen recordings for codewithabel/Disojo
Open-source auto-zoom tools: OpenScreen (MIT), Recordly, Screenize (macOS, Apache-2.0), ScreenArc. Or build our own: record with `ui-demo` (cursor positions known) and generate the zoom/pan/click-highlight in Remotion. Terminal/code sequences should be native React components (typing, syntax colours, per-line delay), not recordings; IBM's developer brand 'undertyping' effect is a good reference.
## Craft principles from sources (vendor blogs, directional)
Punch-ins at emphasis, not on a timer; cutaways must have a job and hide jump cuts; text synced to speech, kinetic type as lead element; restraint: invisible cuts and purposeful effects; vary shot scale; educational/trading = cleaner captions, fewer interruptions. Sound-design sources were thin: use our own tested SFX kit.
## Proposed direction (to confirm after Tejjomay sends his repos)
Engine v5 on Remotion with an Editing Grammar per account: Disojo = calm editorial motion (masked text reveals, wipes, device mockups, diagrams), codewithabel = loud dev-log (terminal/code animation, glitch-free cuts, day counter, screen-recording zoom, acid/cobalt look), trading = restrained (clean captions, chart overlays, purposeful zooms). Spike first: re-cut one existing Disojo reel with transitions + b-roll + graphics, compare to v4 side by side, then decide.

## Pilot result (2026-10-17): Remotion v5 of the Disojo founder reel
`remotion-lab/` (Remotion 4.0.536, local Chrome). Pipeline: `engine/tools/make_base.py` (cut + HDR->SDR + voice clean, no overlays) -> Remotion comp (`src/Reel.tsx`, `Captions.tsx`, `Panels.tsx`, `spec.json`) -> `npx remotion render src/index.ts Reel out/reel-v5.mp4` (about 2m16s for 26.8 s). Output: `edits/disojo-reel-2198-v5-remotion.mp4`, 1080x1920, bt709, -15.8 LUFS, peak -1.5 dBFS (measured; not judged by ear).
New vs v4: spring layout transition (panel slides down while the speaker slides into the lower frame), animated graphics per panel (code tidy-up, seesaw, loop with a travelling dot, rising bars with a drawn line), highlighter via clipped text layers, lower-third name tag, zoom-blur kicks on topic changes, emphasis punch-ins, drifting dot grid + grain, SFX on every transition. Still missing: b-roll cutaways, 3D, whip/mask transitions between scenes, code/terminal and screen-recording components (codewithabel), per-account themes.
