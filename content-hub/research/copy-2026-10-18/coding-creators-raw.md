1. Tangible demo > abstract code: NFC stickers in hand, Oreos as data structures, the app on a phone. First spoken line = show-and-tell.

3. Series with parts ("Vibe coding things in the real world Part 1-3") compound.
4. Stack recipes + keyword freebie = comments (abie.learns 34K/8.6K).
5. Day-N journey alone flops (27-120 likes) unless each day has a specific tension or result.
6. Career/identity anxiety ("college regret", "degrees safe from AI") = the widest reach for developer personal brands.
7. Consistent cover template (Nick) or consistent setting (Jack's desk, NFC props) = recognition.
