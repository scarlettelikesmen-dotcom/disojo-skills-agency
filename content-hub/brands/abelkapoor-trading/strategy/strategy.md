v2, 2026-10-14. Replaces v1 (India/SEBI). Operating model: `../../OPERATING-MODEL.md` (3 posts a day: A talking head, B hot take reel, C carousel). Platforms (5, same handle everywhere, verify limits): TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, YouTube, X. **Completely separate from the Disojo world.** Tradriv and all older reels are discarded.

## 1. Positioning
**The trader who isn't selling you anything.** Behaviour, process and honest review: what actually breaks people (boredom, revenge trades, broken rules, no structure) and the unglamorous habits that fix it (checklists, logon/logoff windows, honest journals). Value first, openly, for 6+ months; the restraint is the signature ("Day N of not selling you anything"). No outcomes promised. Never about what to trade.

## 2. Audience (tier-1 retail traders; validate with comments)
US/UK/Canada/EU retail traders (new to intermediate) who know a setup and still lose to their own behaviour. Their words: overtrading, FOMO, revenge trading, "inconsistent", "blew the account", "journal". Instruments and routes: TO CONFIRM; content stays instrument-agnostic.

## 3. The trio
| Slot | Content |

Weekly series: **Post-mortem #N**, **Rule of the week**, **Day N of not selling you anything**.

## 4. Platform roles (hypotheses; measure)
TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts = discovery (A, B, C where supported). **X** = trading community: B as text-first posts, A video, C image sets; strict no-calls discipline. **Snapchat** = A repurposed only (low).

## 5. Virality plan
Signals: watch time, sends per reach, likes per reach; originality. Recognition hooks ("this is the trade that ruins your day"), send line ("send this to the trader who revenge trades"), a story arc inside 30-40 s, A/B/C hook variants over one body across platforms, a series counter that makes the restraint visible, real face/voice/journal pages. Avoid: income screenshots, win-rate claims, "get profitable" promises, urgency, unsourced stats ("most traders lose").

## 6. Compliance routine (every piece; verify with a securities/financial-promotions lawyer)
UK: FCA financial-promotions regime (FSMA s21) reaches individuals; the FCA has prosecuted finfluencers over forex/CFD schemes. US: FTC (earnings claims, undisclosed material connections), SEC (touting, undisclosed compensation). EU: national rules; stay education-only. Therefore: (1) education only, no recommendations, entries/targets/stop levels as calls, signals, "buy/sell"; (2) no return, win-rate or profit claims, no P&L screenshots; (3) no selling, referral/affiliate links, broker/prop-firm/app promotion, courses; (4) disclaimer on screen and in caption ("Education only. Not investment advice."); (5) no unsourced statistics; (6) nothing from the Disojo world; (7) any "I did X" statement confirmed by him. Claude runs this on every piece and flags failures.

## 7. Measurement
A: avg watch time, 3 s hold, shares, saves. B: reach, follows per reach, profile visits. C: saves, shares, swipe-through, follows. No monetisation metrics for 6 months. 30-day platform review.
