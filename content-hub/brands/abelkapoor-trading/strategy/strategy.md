# Strategy: abelkapoor-trading (value only, no selling for 6+ months)
Draft v1, 2026-10-14. Goal decided by Tejjomay: grow an audience and authority through pure value; **no paid course, no selling, for at least six months**. Compliance research in `../research/sources.md`. Not legal advice: verify with a securities lawyer.

## 1. Positioning
**The trader who isn't selling you anything.** Process, psychology and honest review: what actually makes people lose (boredom, revenge trades, broken rules, no structure) and the unglamorous habits that fix it (checklists, logon/logoff windows, journaling). Value first, openly, with the restraint itself as a signature: people wonder why there is no course, and the answer is the brand ("I'm not selling anything. Judge the content.").
Do not promise outcomes. Do not talk about being profitable. Show process, mistakes and review.

## 2. Audience
Retail traders (early to intermediate) who know a setup and still lose to their own behaviour: "you're up a thousand one day and donate it back the next." Their language comes from Tejjomay's own reels (over-trading, FOMO, revenge trading, stop-loss moves, "inconsistent"). Region and instruments: TO CONFIRM. Validate with comments/DMs.

## 3. Pillars
| Pillar | What it is | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Psychology of mistakes** | Recognition content: over-trading from boredom, revenge trading feeling rational, FOMO entries, moving stop-losses. Always about behaviour, never about what to trade. | 40% |
| **B. Process and rules** | The pre-trade checklist, logon/logoff window, journaling, review loop, risk per trade *as a personal rule* ("a rule I follow"), never as advice to others. | 30% |
| **C. Post-mortems** | One trade-day dissected: what the plan said, what happened, which rule broke. Real or clearly hypothetical. No P&L flexing. | 15% |
| **D. Myths and honesty** | Backtesting is easy, executing is everything; win rate isn't edge; why there's no course. | 10% |
| **E. The not-selling streak** | "Day N of not selling you anything": a counter-series that makes the restraint visible; monthly honest update. | 5% |

## 4. Funnel (no selling)
Reels (reach) -> carousels (saves, sends) -> follow. CTA is only: follow, send to a trader friend, or comment a keyword for a **free** resource (the pre-trade checklist as an image/PDF) delivered without any link to a product. No broker links, no referral codes, no app, no course, no Disojo mention. Monetisation is a separate decision after the six months.

## 5. Virality plan
Signals: watch time, sends per reach, likes per reach (`../../disojo/research/sources.md`).
- **Recognition/identity hooks** with high arousal: "this is the trade that ruins your whole day", "you're not over-trading, you're bored", "revenge trading starts feeling rational". These already exist in Tejjomay's best material.
- **Sends**: end on "send this to the trader friend who revenge trades." Write for the friend who needs to hear it.
- **Watch time**: a story arc inside 30-40 s (setup, "one loss, then another", the turn, the rule). Frame 0 = the uncomfortable truth, on screen and spoken.
- **A/B/C hook variants over one body** (his existing method): film three first lines, post the strongest after testing in stories or the first hours' retention.
- **Series**: Post-mortem #N, Rule of the week, Day N of not selling anything (the counter is the hook).
- **Originality**: his real face and voice, real journal pages; no stock charts of imaginary trades.
- Avoid: income screenshots, win-rate claims, "get profitable" promises, urgency, fear-selling ("90% of traders lose" needs a real source or must go).

## 6. Compliance routine (every post)
Checklist before posting: (1) education only, no recommendations or levels as calls; (2) no return/win-rate/profit claims; (3) no selling, links, referrals, app or course; (4) disclaimer on screen and in caption ("Education only. Not investment advice."); (5) every statistic has a source or is removed; (6) no Disojo/codewithabel/Tradriv anywhere; (7) age/identity per his decision. Claude runs this checklist on every script and flags failures.

## 7. Cadence (proposal; confirm capacity)
**3 reels + 1 carousel per week.** Batch film: 1 session per week, 3 body scripts x 2-3 hook variants (his A/B/C habit). Carousel = a post-mortem or a checklist (designed in the journal look). See `calendar/first-4-weeks.md`.

## 8. Measurement and decisions
Per post: 3-second hold, watch time, sends, follows per reach, saves, comments (quality). No monetisation metrics for six months. After 12 posts keep the pillar with the best sends and follows per reach. A meaningful milestone: consistent posting plus a returning audience (comments that quote his phrases), not follower count.

## 9. Experiments (first 30 days)
1. Recognition hook vs "I used to think..." confession hook.
2. Talking head + journal panels vs voiceover over journal pages.
3. The not-selling streak counter: does it raise follows per reach?
4. 25 s vs 40 s.
5. English vs Hinglish (if comfortable).

## 10. Risks to manage
Regulatory (above), reputational (inflated claims go viral for the wrong reason), personal (age/identity, privacy; see brand TO CONFIRM), burnout (cadence), cross-contamination (any link to the Disojo world or the app breaks the brand promise).
