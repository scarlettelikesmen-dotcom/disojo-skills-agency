# The Wall Model: rules as dictated by Tejjomay (2026-10-15), v1.3 (key time opens + Judas swing added) (all open questions answered by him; two items marked [~] are Claude's interpretation, shown to him)
Concepts are ICT-derived (fair value gaps, liquidity, CISD, inversion, sponsored gaps); the packaging, name and rules are his. Education only.
## Scope
NQ and ES futures. NY AM 9:30-11:00 ET; NY PM 1:30-3:30 ET (PM also used after high-impact news).
## Teaching structure: LEVEL (incl. SMT) -> WALL -> ROAD -> TRIGGER (inversion + CISD + SMT confirmation), plus the skip rule "two walls = no trade". One-liner: "Wall behind the stop, road to the target."
## 1. LEVEL
KEY TIME OPENS (added 2026-10-15; he says they are a big part of the model): 9:30 open and 10:00 open in the NY AM session; 2:00 PM open in the NY PM session. The JUDAS SWING (the false move after the open) is an important key level [~ confirm his exact definition: the fake move after the 9:30 open that reverses]. Earlier notes also listed 8pm and 2am London opens: not in his current list.
Key levels to trade FROM: previous day high/low; weekly and daily FAIR VALUE GAPS; higher-timeframe fair value gaps on 4H, 1H, 15M. Read the overall trend/bias. Take only obvious setups.
Weekly/daily OPENING GAPS are NOT levels to take a trade from. They are high-resistance TARGETS: price tends to fill them before a proper move, so they are good targets.
SMT (divergence between the two indices he trades, NQ vs ES, in either direction; NOT YM) is a BIG key level for him: where one index sweeps or makes a new high/low and the other does not. It is also an extra confirmation factor (see Trigger) and an exit/target tool (below).
## 2. THE WALL (stop side = high resistance)
a) The stop is protected: stop placed exactly at the high or low of the structure where the setup forms (the swing extreme), inside/behind a 15M-or-higher fair value gap [~ confirm: no extra buffer].
b) Continuation model: the entry-timeframe gap must be at least 3M (3M/5M) and SPONSORED: price taps a higher-timeframe gap (e.g. 1H), displaces away, and that displacement leg creates the 3M/5M gap. Then the continuation is valid and the stop is protected. A random 3M/5M gap with no sponsor is invalid.
c) RE-SWEEP (stop raid) when there is an intermediate or major swing high/low on the stop side. Why it works: after price sweeps a key high (or low), it has displaced so much that it cannot displace the other way directly: there is no liquidity there to target. So it moves the other way first, and that move GENERATES low-resistance liquidity (stacked failure swings / equal levels). Then price re-sweeps the key level; now it finally has targets (the liquidity it just generated). More often than not, the first sweep followed by a small move is a trap. After the re-sweep, wait for the CISD/inversion, then the move runs to the generated liquidity. Example (short): sweep the daily intermediate high, dip a little (trap, generating stacked lows), go up again and re-sweep that high, then 1M inversion + CISD, then lower.
## 3. THE ROAD (target side = low resistance)
Target = stacked liquidity: failure swings and relative equal highs/lows (e.g. 5-10 swings in a row that cannot sweep the previous high; for lows, each low higher than the previous). Price goes into a key level and displaces toward that liquidity. Target the NEAREST low-resistance liquidity (low-hanging fruit), not full draws.
## 4. TRIGGER
Key level tapped (or swept) -> 1-minute inversion + CISD (a close significantly beyond the inversion). He always looks for CISD with the 1M inversion. The CISD must be less than 5 points away (closer CISD = better confirmation).
SMT confirmation: preferred, NOT mandatory. About 85% of his trades have SMT (his figure; in content say 'most of my trades', never quote it as a result). In his experience trades without SMT are more likely to fail.
SMT as exit/target: if ES takes the high (or low), he exits on NQ, and vice versa.
## 5. EXECUTION
Aim for about 1 : 1.5 risk:reward. If the nearest target gives about 1:1.4 or 1:1.45 he takes it. If it gives only about 1:1, he aims for the next target and moves to break-even at the nearest one. Position/risk size is not taught.
## 6. NO TRADE
(SMT absence is not a skip rule: it lowers his confidence, it does not forbid the trade.)
Conflicting bias: two higher-timeframe levels opposing (e.g. bouncing off a bullish 1H gap and immediately running into a bearish 4H gap) = high resistance vs high resistance = gambling, skip. Skip chop. High-impact news later in the day: wait and trade after it in the PM session; otherwise do not trade.
