## Compliance guards (India; research notes in `research/sources.md`; verify with a securities lawyer before relying on them)
- **Education only.** No advice, no recommendations, no entries/targets/stop levels as calls, no signals, no "buy/sell this".
- **No return or performance claims** and no win-rate/"0 red months" style statistics. If real results are ever shown: full context (instrument, period, drawdown, costs), backtest vs live clearly separated, and only with a verified record.
- **Disclaimer on every post** ("Education only. Not investment advice.") as a small constant on-screen mark and in the caption.
- No selling, referral links, broker or app promotion, no courses, no "DM me for the link". Paid courses can bring a person within "consideration" tests; free education is the safe lane per SEBI's own consultation response, which excludes persons "solely involved in education... not linked to advice or recommendations" (see sources).
- No income flexing, no screenshots of profits without context.
- Be careful with the word "profitable" and with anything promising outcomes.
- Age/identity: see TO CONFIRM.
