## SEBI / India: education vs advice
- SEBI Investment Advisers Regulations, 2013: advice **for consideration** requires registration; SEBI's stated position (2023, whole-time member) is that payment from any source can trigger registration, while educating free of cost is exempt. Paid courses that disperse advice are a recognised gray area. https://indiacorplaw.in/2024/08/21/sebis-finfluencer-legal-framework-gaps-in-enforcement-and-investor-education/ , https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2023/08/30/power-of-influence-regulating-the-financial-influencers-in-india/ , https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/30556-do-finfluencers-fall-under-sebi-investment-adviser-regulations
- Aug 2024 amendments and 29 Jan 2025 FAQs: **regulated entities (brokers, portfolio managers, investment advisers) may not associate directly or indirectly with unregistered persons who give securities advice or make return claims.** The carve-out: persons **solely engaged in investor education**, not linked to advice or recommendations, are not caught. https://foxmandal.in/news/cutting-ties-with-finfluencers-sebi-distinguishes-between-education-advice/ , https://thefinancestory.com/sebi-new-rules-to-curb-unregistered-finfluencers
- Primary (consultation outcome, July 2024): SEBI said the policy "shall not restrict the association of SEBI regulated entities or their agents with other persons who are solely involved in providing the education or services which are not linked to advice or recommendations related to security or securities." https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/meetingfiles/jul-2024/1719916854117_1.pdf
- Registered finfluencers must disclose registration number and a disclaimer; regulated entities and agents must display their SEBI registration prominently. Unregistered educators: avoid anything that looks like advice or assured returns. https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebi-amends-rules-to-regulate-finfluencers
- SEBI cautions: acting as an investment adviser without registration is illegal; "assured returns" is listed as a malpractice. https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/docfiles/34058_t.html
- Enforcement: reports of 15,000+ pages taken down in 3 months (single outlet; unverified).
- **Not found:** an explicit education exemption in a notified regulation for non-registered individuals' own content; effective dates of every 2026 amendment. Check sebi.gov.in directly and consult a lawyer. Also consider ASCI guidance for financial promotions (not researched).

## Implications used in the strategy
Education only; no advice/recommendations/return claims; no selling (a paid course raises the "consideration" question); disclaimers on every post; avoid unsourced stats; separate world from any app or business.

## UPDATE 2026-10-14 (later): audience is tier-1; SEBI (above) is out of scope. Relevant regimes (secondary sources; verify with the regulators and a lawyer)
- **UK FCA:** under FSMA s21, unauthorised persons may not communicate investment promotions; influencers can be liable even when sharing approved promotions; FCA prosecuted finfluencers (May 2024) over an unauthorised forex scheme, interviewed 20 under caution (Oct 2024), issued dozens of warnings; its March 2024 social-media guidance requires promotions to be fair, clear and not misleading. https://www.financialreporter.co.uk/fca-launches-targeted-action-against-finfluencers.html , https://riskandcompliance.freshfields.com/post/102jmhr/fca-takes-further-steps-to-crackdown-on-finfluencers-following-issuing-guidance , https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2023/global/fca-issues-guidance-on-financial-promotions-on-social-media-platforms
- **US FTC:** deceptive earnings claims (e.g. April 2026 action over income claims) and undisclosed material connections; disclose any relationship clearly. https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/ftc-brings-second-earnings-claims-case-6496816/ , https://terms.law/Trading-Legal/guides/ftc-endorsement-guidelines.html
- **US SEC:** touting rules; disclose nature, source and amount of compensation for promotions (ICO cases). Same FTC link above.
- Takeaway: education only, no return claims, no undisclosed affiliations, no selling.
