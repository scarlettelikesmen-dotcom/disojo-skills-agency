# Hook bank v3: @abelkapoor (authority-first; model-based; no results claims)
Model name (LOCKED 2026-10-15): The Wall Model. Rules for every hook: a plain declarative line or a reframe, 5-10 words on screen, no income/win-rate claims.
## A: talking head (30-40 s)
1. Every trade I take has a wall behind my stop.
2. You don't need more indicators. You need a clear path to your target.
3. Most traders mark the wrong levels. Here's what actually matters.
4. High resistance behind the stop. Low resistance to the target.
5. CISD in 30 seconds.
6. Why the 1-minute inversion works, and when it doesn't.
7. Before every entry I check one thing: is the road open?
8. Stop overcomplicating the 1-minute chart.
9. The difference between a setup and a hope.
10. Why your stop gets hit and your target doesn't.
## B: hot takes (chart screen-recording or b-roll + trending audio; tiny caption)
1. More information won't fix your execution.
2. If three levels sit between you and your target, it's not a target.
3. Put your stop where the market has to work to reach it.
4. Simple isn't easy. It's just clear.
5. You don't need to catch every move.
6. A level is only a level if price respects it.
7. The 1-minute chart isn't random. Your context was.
8. Skip more trades. Take better ones.
9. Teach the model. Hide nothing.
10. The market pays patience, not activity.
11. Mark the level. Check the road. Then wait.
12. Complexity is how you hide not knowing.
