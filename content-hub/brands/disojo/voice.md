## Two registers
- **Brand (carousels, captions, replies):** authoritative, calm, structured, respectful, sovereign. Formal address ("Dear Kunal,") in client comms; in public content, plain and specific. Motto: "Peace of mind is our forte."
- **Founder on camera (reels):** direct, conversational, blunt when it earns its place. Real lines from the take: "agencies are just trash at building good software", "we care about your business more than the money", "when you get business, that's when we get business", "our whole goal is to give you more business".

## Rules
- Specific over abstract: name the actual pain (a confirmation, a reschedule, "is 5pm free?").
- Never submissive or desperate: no "sorry for the delay", "please let me know if the price is okay", "we will do anything".
- Honest about limits: "sometimes you don't need custom software" is on-brand.
- No hype words ("game changer", "revolutionary"), no fake urgency, no growth-bar clichés.
- Blunt lines about competitors ("trash") are opinion and Tejjomay's call; the calm register is the default. Flag them.
- No invented numbers or claims; client results only "as reported by the client".
