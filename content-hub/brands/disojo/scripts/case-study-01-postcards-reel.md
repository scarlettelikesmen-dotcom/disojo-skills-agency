# Postcards reel: strategy and full plan (DRAFT, 2026-11-18, waiting on Tejjo)
Research behind it: `brands/disojo/research/agency-reels-2026-11-18.md` (27 accounts).

## The strategy in five moves
1. **Collab post with @postcardsperfumes.** The single biggest lever in the research: almost every outlier had a second audience. Postcards' followers are perfume buyers and their friends, and the post sits on both grids. Needs the client's OK and their tap on the invite.
2. **Open on something physical, in the first frame.** A rubber stamp slamming the arch mark onto kraft. No logo fade-in, no title card.
3. **Tell it as a problem with a turn, in under 8 words a card:** "People can't smell a website." / "So we sold the place." That line is the idea of the whole job and it is shareable on its own.
4. **Show how it was made, then the result.** Mark, label, bottle, postcard, store, box, number. Every cut lands on the beat Tejjo picks in-app.
5. **Built to loop and to be sent.** The last frame is the stamp lifting off the box, which is the first frame, so it replays cleanly. Caption ends with a send line, and a comment keyword for the case study.

## Built for virality AND conversion (added on Tejjo's ask)
The two pull in different directions: perfume buyers share, business owners book. Reach comes from Postcards' audience and the craft; conversion comes from owners seeing their own problem and the number.

**Retention (what keeps people watching and replaying)**
- Frame 1 is already moving (stamp mid-fall), never black, never a logo fade.
- A new picture every 1-1.5 s in the first 8 s, then longer holds on the store; every cut on the beat.
- On-screen text: max 7 words a card, top third, inside the Reels safe zone (clear of the caption and buttons at the bottom 20 percent).
- The number lands at 20 s, so people who stay get the payoff; the stamp loop sends them round again (replays count as watch time).
- 28-32 s total: long enough to tell it, short enough to finish.

**Shares and sends**
- The hook line is a thought people repeat: "People can't smell a website."
- Caption send line aimed at the right person: "Send this to someone who sells something you have to touch."
- Postcards reposts it to their story and pins it; their customers send it to friends.

**Conversion (owners who could hire us)**
- Hook variants tested with Instagram trial reels (shown to non-followers first), same edit, only the first card changes:
  1. "People can't smell a website." (curiosity, broad: virality)
  2. "Their ads were losing money. We fixed the store, not the ads." (owner pain: conversion)
  3. "Every 1 spent on ads came back as 3.70." (result first: conversion)
  The winner on 3 s hold and sends goes out as the main post.
- Comment keyword POSTCARDS sends a DM (ManyChat, or by hand for now) with: the case study page, then one line, "Want the same look at your store? 15 minutes: cal.com/disojo-studio/discovery-call".
- Profile ready before posting: the carousel pinned next to the reel, bio line naming the result type we deliver, Client Results highlight. Profile visits are where owners decide.
- No ₹ or Indian references in our words (tier-1 audience); the client's store shows its own prices, which is fine.

**Distribution**
- Post when the US East Coast and UK overlap (about 13:00-15:00 London). Confirm with our own Insights once there are a few posts.
- Same day: native uploads to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn (company page) with a different first line and cover each, no watermarks.
- Cover: the stamped mark on kraft (matches the carousel's tile 1, so the grid reads as one case study).
- Caption keeps searchable words: perfume brand, packaging design, brand identity, ecommerce store.

**What we measure, and what we do with it (48 h and 7 days, log in published/log.md)**
- 3 s hold under 60 percent: the first card or first frame changes.
- Average watch under 12 s: cut the middle (the store section) shorter.
- Sends per reach is the reach signal; keyword comments, DMs and booked calls are the conversion signal. A reel with fewer views but more calls wins.
