# Research sources: @disojo.solutions (read 2026-10-14)
Caveat: most sources are vendor/marketing blogs; numbers are indicative, not verified. Re-check anything before relying on it.

## Instagram mechanics (applies to all three accounts)
- Confirmed ranking signals (Adam Mosseri, Jan 2025): **watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach**. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers; likes per reach matter more for followers. Saves: sources disagree, not on the official list. Originality is favoured (Mosseri's Dec 2025 memo reportedly prioritises raw human content over AI-generated in 2026; reposts/watermarks reportedly demoted; the "10+ reposts" claim is unverified).
  Sources: https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works-in-2026 , https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works , https://www.inro.social/guides/instagram-algorithm-guide , https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/
- Reels reach non-followers; first 3 seconds decide the swipe (multiple blogs; the "half drop off before 4 s" figure is unverified).

## B2B / agency Instagram
- Behind-the-scenes content builds trust; for leads use clear CTAs and a prominent contact path in the bio; Reels reach vs carousel engagement figures come from one vendor blog (indicative only: 30.81% reach rate for Reels, 10.15% engagement for carousels).
  Sources: https://www.tripledart.com/blog/instagram-for-b2b-marketing , https://www.oktopost.com/blog/instagram-b2b-marketing/ , https://socialk.it/en/blog/instagram-for-b2b , https://www.marketingprofs.com/articles/2023/49775/how-to-get-b2b-leads-from-instagram?e=4
- **Gap:** no verified case study of an Indian software agency winning leads from Instagram. Treat the strategy as an experiment.

## Owner-run business pain points (India)
- Manual invoicing via WhatsApp (notebook -> billing app -> PDF -> WhatsApp), slow collections, GST detail errors; packaged tools (Vyapar, Tally integrations, Swipe) already cover invoicing, reminders and UPI links. Implication: be honest that custom software is for the workflows packaged tools do not handle (your own fee structure, booking rules, records). WhatsApp automation needs the official API via a Business Solution Provider.
  Sources: https://tallysolutions.com/business-guides/whatsapp-invoicing-payments-india-2026/ , https://vyaparapp.in/invoicing-software , https://getswipe.in/blog/collections/can-you-send-professional-invoices-through-whatsapp
- Gap: little found on fee tracking or bookings specifically; learn from real DMs and calls.
