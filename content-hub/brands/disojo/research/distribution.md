# Research: multi-platform distribution (read 2026-10-14). Mostly secondary/vendor sources; verify on each platform's own policy page.
- **YouTube** (monetisation policy, 15 Jul 2025): "repetitious" became "inauthentic content"; mass-produced, templated videos with only superficial differences are exposed; reused material is allowed if it adds original value (commentary, editing, narration); clear human contribution is safest. https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155 , https://www.contentgrip.com/youtube-tightens-monetisation/ , https://www.subsub.io/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-2025
- **Instagram**: accounts that primarily post unoriginal content (reposts incl. photos/carousels) are no longer shown in recommendation surfaces; watermarks, speed changes and credits do not make a repost original; watermarked TikTok reposts are the classic case; export clean or recreate natively. https://www.emarketer.com/content/instagram-s-algorithm-clamps-down-on-repurposed--unoriginal-photos-posts , https://routenote.com/blog/instagram-tightens-rules-reposted-content/ , https://www.planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy.md
- **TikTok**: reuploads from other platforms reportedly detected and made ineligible for the For You feed; edit per app; "Content Check Lite" (Jul 2025) previews eligibility; no source found on posting the same content across multiple accounts. https://socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm , https://onlineoptimism.com/blog/updates-tiktoks-community-guidelines
- **LinkedIn**: native video beats links (links reportedly cut reach); 30-90 s captioned talking-head videos from personal profiles; hook in the first seconds; test vertical vs landscape; vendor numbers (85% muted, 3x reach) unverified. https://shootsta.com/blog/linkedin-video-best-practices-b2b-teams , https://ligosocial.com/en/blog/linkedin-video-post-best-practices , https://www.visla.us/blog/?p=6715
- **Snapchat Spotlight**: monetisation is invitation-only; one 2026 guide lists 50,000 followers, 15,000 view-hours in 28 days, Snap Star status, posting on 10 of the last 28 days; fully AI-generated videos removed from the payout pool. Check Snapchat's own program page. https://creatorsagency.co/blog/snapchat-monetization-requirements-2026 , https://www.shortsync.app/resources/snapchat-spotlight-monetization-2026
- **Gap:** no verified source on limits per platform (caption lengths, video max lengths) or on posting identical content across platforms from one creator. Verify before building export presets.
