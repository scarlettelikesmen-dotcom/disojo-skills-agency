# Case study series (DRAFT 2026-11-18, waiting on Tejjo's answers)

Reference: @nonstopbranding grid (Tejjo's screenshot, 2026-11-18). Their grid is built so each client fills one row of three, all in that client's colour:
1. the client's mark on its brand colour (a carousel that opens into the identity),
2. a process or mockup carousel,
3. a reel (screen recording or product film).
Two pinned posts are testimonials. The grid itself works as the portfolio.

What carries over: each client gets one row of three, the grid as a portfolio, and testimonials pinned.
What does not: their tiles start with a logo they designed. Most of ours are websites and systems. Opening a row with a client logo we did not draw would suggest we did. So tile 1 shows what we actually made.

## One row per client = 3 posts
| Tile | Format | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Carousel, 7 slides: the case study | 1 cover (client name + the one verified result) / 2 before / 3 the idea / 4-5 the build (real screens) / 6 the result, "as reported by the client" / 7 one CTA |
| 2 | Reel, 15-25 s | Screen recording of the live site, cut in the locked v9 style (punch zooms, panels, word captions). Or Tejjo on camera over it |
| 3 | Carousel or single image | The detail: the system behind it (portal, CRM, automation), or the client's words in a quote card |

Posting order: one client's row a week, in the order 3, 2, 1, so the row reads right on the grid. Fits the 3-a-day model as C (case study), B (reel), C (detail).
