## The one sentence
**Disojo is a software and consulting studio for founder-led and owner-run businesses. We sit down with you, find what is holding the business back, and build the systems that fix it.**

Short form (bios, footers): *We find what's holding your business back, then build what fixes it.*

## What we are, and what we are not
- We are: the people who understand the business first, then build. Diagnosis and delivery from the same team, led by the founder.
- We are not: a dev shop that takes a spec and writes code, or consultants who hand over slides and leave.
- "Consulting" means: we map how the business really runs (where the work leaks, what is retyped, chased, remembered, guessed) before anything is designed or built. It is not sold separately.

## Who it is for
- **Founder-led** businesses (newer, startup-minded) and **owner-run** businesses (established, old-school). Same problem, different words: the business grew, the way it runs and looks did not.
- **Global:** India and abroad. Decided 2026-11-02 (replaces the tier-1-only note in `brand.md`).
- Write so both get it: plain English, no jargon. If a term like "MVP" appears, the sentence around it explains it.

## What a founder should feel after one visit
"These people know what they're doing. They understood what I'm struggling with before they tried to sell me anything, and they can fix it."
Every page is judged against that sentence.

## The offer
1. **Strategy consultation, free.** We map the business and find the bottlenecks. If software will not help, we say so.
2. **We build in stages.** You see working screens as they appear; no big reveal at the end.
3. **The founder leads every project** (Tejjo). You deal with the founder, not a sales team.
4. **We stay on after launch.**

- MVPs for startups
- Internal business tools (admin panels, CRMs, dashboards, portals)
- Digital systems and automation

## Voice: how "they know what they're doing" sounds
- **Diagnose before prescribing.** Name the exact pain first (a confirmation chased by message, a sheet retyped every Monday), then what changed.
- **Specific, calm, short.** Declarative sentences. Numbers only when verified.
- **Talk about the business, not the tech.** No stack names in sales copy.
- **Confident, never submissive.** No "sorry", no "we'd love to", no "please let us know if".
- **Honest limits are a strength.** "If software won't help, we'll tell you."
- **Never:** hype words (cutting-edge, game changer, revolutionary, seamless, leverage, solutions, unlock, elevate), em dashes, invented numbers, insulting other agencies in site copy, "vibe-coded".
- Visible copy says Bombay; titles, meta and legal say Mumbai.
