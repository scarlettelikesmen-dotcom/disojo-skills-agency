## 2. What makes it a flagship

What the market shows (from the references):
- Softriver sells a logo, a guidelines deck and mockups from $280. Its Trussly deck and the Uprise deck in the Nino reel share the same page list and layout, so they are probably the same template.
- Andrija Designs sells strategy, 2 moodboards, 2 concepts, a 50+ page book and 2 revision rounds for €5,000. That is branding only, with no site.

So a guidelines deck with billboard mockups is the floor, not the flagship. Ours stands on four things:
1. **An idea only this client could own.** Three directions in words from what is true about their business, before any pixel. Not a clean logo on a stock billboard.
2. **A book made for this brand.** Its layout comes from the identity, never a template.
3. **The whole presence in one go.** Brand, website, every channel and launch content, made together, so nothing looks borrowed from another stage.
4. **A process the client can see.** Numbered steps, a portal, something to look at at the end of every step.

## 3. What each price buys

### Branding, $4,000 (SETTLED 2026-11-22: the split below)
- A short strategy: positioning, audience, voice in a page or two. From a questionnaire and one call.
- The idea: three directions in words, the client picks one.
- The identity: mark, type, colour, the one device the identity repeats.
- A core guidelines book, about 20 pages.
- The brand on 3 of the client's real things (they choose: sign, packaging, card, merch, uniform, menu).
- Final files: SVG, PNG, PDF, in colour, ink and white.

Not included (flagship only): the founder workshop, the full brand world, motion, the digital presence kit, launch content, the website.

Upgrade path (PROPOSED): a branding client who comes back for the site within `___` months pays the flagship price minus `___`. Tejjo to decide if there is any credit at all.

## 4. The client journey, numbered

What the client sees. Each step ends with something to look at and an approval.

| # | Step | The client sees | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Portal** | One place for files, stages, invoices, messages. | none |
| 2 | **Questionnaire and workshop** | The questionnaire, then a founder workshop (flagship) or one call (branding). | none |
| 3 | **Strategy** | One page: who it is for, what it says, how it sounds. | signs off |
| 4 | **The idea** | Three directions in words, with boards. | picks one |
| 5 | **Identity** | Mark, type, colour, shown on their real things. | approves |
| 6 | **Guidelines book** | The book, page by page. | approves |
| 7 | **Website** (flagship) | First screen on a demo link, then the full site. | approves each |
| 8 | **Digital presence** (flagship) | Profiles set up, templates, the 9 posts and 3 reels. | approves |
| 9 | **Launch** (flagship) | The site live, profiles switched, launch posts ready. | says go |
| 10 | **Handover** | Final files, the book, logins, a short walkthrough. Retainer offer. | none |

Branding runs steps 1 to 6, then 10.

Inside Disojo, Tejjo picks before the client sees anything at steps 4, 5 and 7 (the idea-before-pixels rule).
