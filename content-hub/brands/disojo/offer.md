## The buyer
A founder-led business that is already doing good numbers, where one or both of these is true:
1. **The business runs on people, chats and spreadsheets.** The founder is the bottleneck. Bookings, payments, follow-ups and reports are chased by hand.
2. **The business looks smaller than it is.** The identity, site and presence match the business from three years ago, not its ambition.

Qualifier, Tejjo to set: `___` (candidates: yearly revenue band, team size, years operating, already paying for ads). Outreach needs a filter, so one is needed before the first list is built.

Not for: pre-revenue ideas (MVPs only by exception, when funded), people who want to hand over a spec and get code, template buyers.

## The promise (one sentence)
**We find where your business leaks time and looks smaller than it is, then build the systems and identity that fix both, in stages you can see.**

## Two doors
| | Door A: Presence | Door B: Bottleneck fix |
|---|---|---|
| Fixes | The business looks smaller than it is | The work runs on hand-work and the founder |
| Output | Visual identity, site, enquiry pipeline | A custom system: portal, admin panel, CRM, dashboard, automation |
| Proof | Ascender Media, Postcards Perfumes | KDC, Mentable |
| Entry symptom the buyer says out loud | "Our site embarrasses us in front of bigger clients" | "I'm the one who knows how everything works" |

Both doors can run in sequence for one client. No bundle discount: scope changes, rates do not (AGENTS.md section 2.1).

## The process (same for both doors)
0. **Diagnosis, free** (decided 2026-11-02). Make it a defined thing, so it is not endless free consulting: a qualifying form, then one call of `___` minutes, then a **written one-page Leak Map** sent within `___` days. If software won't help, the Leak Map says so.
1. **Proposal:** fixed scope, fixed price, named stages.
2. **Build in stages.** Every stage ends with something the client can see or use, a review, then the stage payment. No big reveal.
3. **Launch and handover.**
4. **Stay on.** Retainer, optional.

### Stages (proposed, adjust)
- **Door A:** 1) Direction: three identity directions, client picks one or a mix. 2) Identity locked, first screen of the site on a demo link. 3) Full site on the demo link. 4) Enquiry pipeline live: form to tracked lead to notification. Launch.
- **Door B:** 1) The work mapped as it really runs. 2) First clickable screen on real data. 3) Core flows live on staging, used by the client's own team. 4) Roll-out and training. Launch, then a watch period of `___` days.
