## The buyer
A founder-led business that is already doing good numbers, where one or both of these is true:
1. **The business runs on people, chats and spreadsheets.** The founder is the bottleneck. Bookings, payments, follow-ups and reports are chased by hand. (Offer A)
2. **The business looks smaller than it is.** The identity, site and presence match the business from three years ago, not its ambition. (Offer B)

Qualifier, Tejjo to set: `___` (candidates: yearly revenue band, team size, years operating, already paying for ads). Outreach needs one filter before the first list is built.

Not for: pre-revenue ideas (MVPs only by exception, when funded), people who want to hand over a spec and get code, template buyers.

## The process (same for both)
0. **Diagnosis, free** (decided 2026-11-02). Make it a defined thing: a qualifying form, then one call of `___` minutes, then a **written one-page Leak Map** sent within `___` days. If software won't help, the Leak Map says so.
1. **Proposal:** fixed scope, fixed price, named stages.
2. **Build in stages.** Every stage ends with something the client can see or use, a review, then the stage payment. No big reveal.
3. **Launch and handover.**
4. **Stay on.** Retainer, optional.

### Stages (proposed, adjust)
- **Offer A:** 1) The work mapped as it really runs. 2) First clickable screen on real data. 3) Core flows live on staging, used by the client's own team. 4) Roll-out and training. Launch, then a watch period of `___` days.
- **Offer B:** 1) Direction: three identity directions, client picks one or a mix. 2) Identity locked, first screen of the site on a demo link. 3) Full site on the demo link. 4) Enquiry pipeline live: form to tracked lead to notification. Launch.

**Payment schedule:** the standard 20 / 20 / 40 / 20 from AGENTS.md section 2.2.

## Pricing rules I recommend (your call)
1. **Price Offer A against the leak.** The Leak Map puts a cost on the problem using the client's own numbers: hours a week, who does them, bookings missed. The fee is a fraction of that. KDC's "10 hours back every week" is the model. Never put a cost in a Leak Map that the client did not give you.
2. **Decide whether to publish a starting price.** Open since 2026-11-02. My lean: publish "from $___" for Offer B only (more comparable between businesses) and keep Offer A as a quoted price after the Leak Map.
3. **A large Offer A needs a way above the top of its range.** Multi-role systems with many integrations can pass $8,000. Suggest "$5,000 to $8,000, larger systems quoted after the Leak Map". General market knowledge, not verified: US agencies often price custom internal tools well above this range.
