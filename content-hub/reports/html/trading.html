2. The five things that actually build trust fast

  1. Generosity you can feel. Evan's biggest reel credits OTHER teachers. Lists, checklists and next steps that are complete. Your "why is this free?" positioning is the strongest card in the niche because almost nobody can play it.
  2. A named model taught as a series. "Asia ATM strikes again", "Wick Theory", "TTP model". Repetition of one name makes you the owner of an idea. The Wall Model is perfect for this.
  3. Showing the losses and break-evens. Charlie and PB post them in the same format as wins. That is why their wins are believed.
  4. A real human story. Origin and identity reels (job, doubts, first payout) get 100K+ even on tiny accounts. People trust a person, then a model.
  5. Volume and consistency. Evan's reach per reel was flat (median 9K) for 8 weeks. The followers came from 3-4 posts a day plus a few spikes, not from any single viral hit.

3. The flywheel (Evan's loop, adapted)

Reach reels (story, POV, lists) -> profile visits -> pinned "whole model" reel + clean bio -> follow -> daily Wall Model education (saves, questions) -> free Discord (month 2+) -> questions answered on camera (more content) -> proof drip (your real payouts, later student wins only if real and consented) -> Psyche Society at $49/mo (month 5-6) -> members' questions and results feed more content -> more reach. The difference from Evan: no lifestyle-as-income, no win-rate, and the model itself stays free forever. You sell correction, review and accountability, never the model.

4. How to frame every piece

  • You are a teacher who trades, not a trader who flexes. The chart is the hero; money is a footnote.
  • One idea per reel. Every A reel teaches one rule of Level / Wall / Road / Trigger / No-trade.
  • Concrete beats clever. NQ, ES, 9:30, 10:00, stop, wall, road, CISD, 5 points. Numbers only if true.
  • Calm certainty + honest humility. "This is my rule." "It still fails sometimes."
  • Credit ICT. The concepts are ICT's. The packaging, rules and name are yours.
  • Raw production. Trading-lite edit by default (one line of text, your face, your chart). The Rulebook panels are for model explainers and carousels.

7b. Twelve full A scripts (25-40 s)

  1. Rule: the Wall (#4). "Every trade I take has a wall behind my stop. My stop sits at the high or low of the structure, inside a 15-minute or higher gap. That gap is the wall: price has to work hard to get through it. No wall, no trade. Save this video."
  2. Next-step (#2). "You just passed your eval. Here's what to do next. One: don't change anything. The rules that passed it are the rules that keep it. Two: size down for the first week. Three: journal every trade, even the boring ones. Do the boring one first. Save this video." (TO CONFIRM you agree with all three)
  3. The re-sweep (#11/#4). "The first sweep is usually a trap. Price sweeps a key high and drops. Most people short it. But there's nothing below to target yet. So it dips, builds stacked lows, then goes back and re-sweeps the high. Now it has somewhere to run. I wait for the re-sweep, then the CISD. Save this video."
  4. Road (#11). "I don't target the big move. I target the easy one. Stacked lows, failure swings, equal highs: that's low resistance. I aim for about 1 to 1.5. If the nearest target only gives me 1 to 1, I go for the next one and move to break-even at the first. Save this video."
  5. Trigger (#8). "CISD in 30 seconds. Price taps my level. I drop to the 1-minute and wait for an inversion. Then a close clearly beyond it: that's the CISD. I want it less than 5 points away. Closer means a tighter stop. Save this video."
  6. Time (#9/#12). "Price tells you where. Time tells you when. I watch three opens: 9:30, 10:00, and 2:00 in the afternoon. The first move after the open often fakes you out. That's the Judas swing. I let it happen, then I look for my level. Save this video."
  7. SMT (#12). "NQ and ES disagreeing is information. If NQ makes a new low and ES doesn't, that's SMT. Most of my trades have it. And I use it to get out: if ES takes the high, I'm out on NQ. Save this video."
  8. No trade (#7). "Two walls. No trade. Price bounces off a bullish 1-hour gap and runs straight into a bearish 4-hour gap. High resistance on both sides. That's not a setup. That's a coin flip. I skip it. Skipping is part of the model."
