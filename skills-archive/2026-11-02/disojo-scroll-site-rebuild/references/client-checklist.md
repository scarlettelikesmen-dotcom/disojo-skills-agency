# Before building for a client, and before they see it

## Ask the client for
1. Straight-on photos of every real bottle/product (label readable) plus one shelf photo for true cap/label look.
2. Exact label wording for every SKU (notes line, size line). Copy it character for character.
3. Product list with real prices, sizes, and the store platform (Shopify variant ids if buying through their store).
4. Real testimonials with permission, real Instagram handle, real contact email, real policies.
5. Their brand colours/fonts if they have a kit. Otherwise propose an editorial pairing (Fraunces + Plus Jakarta Sans here).
6. Who receives form submissions (B2B enquiries, newsletter) and where.

## Do not ship until
- No invented reviews, stats, offers or products. (Postcards: a "Discovery Set" testimonial referenced a product that did not
  exist in the rebuild; sample packs were left out of the quiz result rather than invent a price.)
- Contact emails are consistent everywhere (the rebuild had two different ones).
- Forms: mailto links are placeholders. Wire a real backend or tell the client plainly they are not wired.
- AI footage is compared with the real product (cap, label spelling, colours). Disclose AI-generated assets and SynthID.
- Tested on a real phone, not only headless Chrome.
- Reduced-motion path works; keyboard focus visible; text is real DOM text.
- Repo has a remote (the Postcards build lived only on one Mac for a long time).
