Tejjo is shaping a high-ticket service: redo a client's brand identity and online appearance ("insane" quality), branding + bespoke website, with optional 3D assets. Price range stated by Tejjo on 2026-10-14: **USD 10-15k** (USD confirmed in chat; still no public pricing).

Reference sites Tejjo wants the quality of: oharchitecture.com.au, mammothmurals.com, supersolid.agency, slik.com.au, hiss.sydney, backhouse.com, squiggle.sydney. Studied on desktop (1382x811) and phone (343x811). All seven are Webflow + GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText, CustomEase) + Lenis, with shared ease curves (0.83,0,0.17,1 and 0.16,1,0.3,1; durations 0.3-0.75s).
