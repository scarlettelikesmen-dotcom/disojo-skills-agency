Tejjomay, 2026-11-08, for all site work (added to skill [[project-site-system]] Hard rules):

1. **Never a one-pager.** Every site Disojo builds is multi-page: home + real inner pages (about, work/catalog, product/service detail, contact, legal). A single landing page or one long scroll is not a deliverable, even for a "simple" brief.
2. **Never basic/dead CTAs or plain boring components.** No plain no-motion buttons, no undesigned links/cards/rows. Design the interaction layer (button, link, card, row, menu, cursor, reveals) from the brand's idea BEFORE composing sections. Don't make Tejjomay sit and ask for the basics — "you know what we do."
3. **The bar is to be BETTER than the nine reference sites**: bymonolog.com + the eight studios in its "our work" section (OH Architecture, Mammoth Murals, Supersolid, SLIK, HISS, Backhouse, Squiggle, plus Ciao/Palmo-tier 3D). Plus a couple of our own (disojostudio.com). **Print Elements is NOT a reference** and neither are our earlier builds — those nine are.

**Why:** our builds keep passing gates and still landing under the references (VÆL, Mentable v1); the gap is composition + interaction craft, not "working". See [[gates-are-not-quality]], [[feedback-premium-build-process]], [[feedback-unique-design-per-client]].

**How to apply:** start every site from skill `disojo-site-system`; teardown the 9 refs, build the interaction library first, split into pages, side-by-side vs the refs before showing.
